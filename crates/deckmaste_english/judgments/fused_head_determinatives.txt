# Fused determiner-heads: plural demonstrative *those*, anaphoric or set by a
# relative clause (CGEL, Ch. 5 §9.2, p. 414, [14i]: *Those who break the
# rules*), and *either* as an implicit partitive (§9.3, p. 413, [11ii]).
+ Exile all creatures except those that share a creature type with a creature that convoked this spell, all artifacts, and all enchantments. => NounPhrase "all creatures except those that share a creature type with a creature that convoked this spell"; RelativeClause "that share a creature type with a creature that convoked this spell"; NounPhrase "all artifacts"; NounPhrase "all enchantments"
+ <card=Worms of the Earth> At the beginning of each upkeep, any player may sacrifice two lands of their choice or have this enchantment deal 5 damage to that player. If a player does either, destroy this enchantment. => NounPhrase "either" as Object of do
# Fused *either* and *neither* are ordinary singular Noun Phrases, so they are Complements too.
+ Exile either. => NounPhrase "either" as Object of exile
+ Exile either of them. => NounPhrase "either of them" as Object of exile; readings 1
