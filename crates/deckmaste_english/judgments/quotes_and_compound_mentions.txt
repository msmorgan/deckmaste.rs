# Quoted text and cited words as constituents. A quoted imperative names the additional cost it states, a singular count noun under a determiner (CGEL, Ch. 5 §8.2, p. 401; Ch. 20 §6, p. 1753; [CR#118.8]); a quoted ability follows *text* as its integrated apposition (Ch. 5 §14.3, p. 447); a cited noun premodified by a cited word is a composite nominal (Ch. 5 §14.4, pp. 448-450); a Subject headed by *type* is the variable of a specifying copular clause that names its value (Ch. 4 §5.5, pp. 266-269). Nested single quotes in a quoted ability need no engine support (Urza's Saga, chapter II).
+ <card=Brutal Suppression> Activated abilities of nontoken Rebels cost an additional "Sacrifice a land" to activate. => NounPhrase "an additional \"Sacrifice a land\"" as Object of cost; Clause "Sacrifice a land"
+ <card=Drought> Spells cost an additional "Sacrifice a Swamp" to cast for each black mana symbol in their mana costs. => NounPhrase "an additional \"Sacrifice a Swamp\"" as Object of cost; Clause "Sacrifice a Swamp"
+ <card=Volrath's Shapeshifter> As long as the top card of your graveyard is a creature card, this creature has the full text of that card and has the text "{2}: Discard a card." => QuotedAbility "\"{2}: Discard a card.\"" as Complement of text
+ <card=Teferi's Realm> At the beginning of each player's upkeep, that player chooses artifact, creature, land, or non-Aura enchantment. => Mention "artifact, creature, land, or non-Aura enchantment" as Object of choose; Mention "non-Aura enchantment"; MentionModifier "non-Aura" as Modifier of enchantment
+ <card=Sarpadian Empires, Vol. VII> As this artifact enters, choose white Citizen, blue Camarid, black Thrull, red Goblin, or green Saproling. => Mention "white Citizen, blue Camarid, black Thrull, red Goblin, or green Saproling" as Object of choose; Mention "white Citizen"; Mention "green Saproling"
+ <card=Artificial Evolution> The new creature type can't be Wall. => NounPhrase "The new creature type" as Subject of can
+ <card=Urza's Saga> III — Search your library for an artifact card with mana cost {0} or {1}, put it onto the battlefield, then shuffle. => PrepositionPhrase "with mana cost {0} or {1}"
