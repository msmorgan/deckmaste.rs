# "enter as a copy of ...": the as-phrase is a predicative adjunct of enter (CGEL Ch. 4 §5.3, p. 263); enter selects no as-phrase
+ Creatures you control enter as a copy of this creature. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of this creature" as Adjunct of enter; NounPhrase "a copy of this creature" as PredicativeComplement of as; !PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of this creature" as Complement of enter
+ You may have this creature enter as a copy of a creature you control. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of a creature you control" as Adjunct of enter
+ You may have this land enter tapped as a copy of any land on the battlefield. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any land on the battlefield" as Adjunct of enter
+ You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature card in a graveyard. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any creature card" as Adjunct of enter || PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any creature card in a graveyard" as Adjunct of enter
+ You may have this Equipment enter as a copy of any Equipment on the battlefield. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any Equipment on the battlefield" as Adjunct of enter
