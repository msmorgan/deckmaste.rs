# An "except" preposition phrase with a clause complement as an Adjunct of the verb "enter" in copy-replacement text
+ <card=Evil Twin> You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature on the battlefield, except it has "{U}{B}, {T}: Destroy target creature with the same name as this creature." => PrepositionPhrase "except it has \"{U}{B}, {T}: Destroy target creature with the same name as this creature.\"" as Adjunct of may

# *In addition to its other types* modifies the predicate *be*, never the
# type nominal it follows.
+ <card=Activated Sleeper> You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature card in a graveyard that was put there from the battlefield this turn, except it's a Phyrexian in addition to its other types. => PrepositionPhrase "in addition to its other types" as Adjunct of be; !Nominal "Phyrexian in addition to its other types"
