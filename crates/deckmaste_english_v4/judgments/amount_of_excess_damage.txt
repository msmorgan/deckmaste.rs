# *excess* premodifies *damage* in *the amount of excess damage dealt this way*: a relational noun keeps its selected Complement as a bare count nominal after a preposition (*in excess of*; CGEL, Ch. 7 §3.1, pp. 618–620), so *of* never takes bare *excess* with *damage dealt this way* as a relative clause (oracle-style-guide §4, "Number, amount, total, much, and many"; §12, "Damage")
