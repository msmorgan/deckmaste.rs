# "enter as a copy of ..." : the as-phrase with a nominal predicative complement is a selected complement of enter, not an adjunct
+ Creatures you control enter as a copy of this creature. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of this creature" as Complement of enter; NounPhrase "a copy of this creature" as PredicativeComplement of as; !PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of this creature" as Adjunct of enter
+ You may have this creature enter as a copy of a creature you control. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of a creature you control" as Complement of enter
+ You may have this land enter tapped as a copy of any land on the battlefield. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any land" as Complement of enter || PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any land on the battlefield" as Complement of enter
+ You may have this creature enter as a copy of any creature card in a graveyard. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any creature card" as Complement of enter || PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any creature card in a graveyard" as Complement of enter
+ You may have this Equipment enter as a copy of any Equipment on the battlefield. => PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any Equipment" as Complement of enter || PrepositionPhrase "as a copy of any Equipment on the battlefield" as Complement of enter
+ Creatures you control enter tapped.
+ You may have this creature enter tapped.
+ Activate as a sorcery. => PrepositionPhrase "as a sorcery" as Adjunct of activate
+ Corrupted — For each opponent who has three or more poison counters as you cast this spell, put up to one target creature card from that player's graveyard onto the battlefield under your control. => PrepositionPhrase "as you cast this spell"; Clause "you cast this spell" as Complement of as
+ Each other creature you control enters with a number of additional +1/+1 counters on it equal to this creature's power and as a Mutant in addition to its other types. => PrepositionPhrase "with a number of additional +1/+1 counters on it equal to this creature's power"
