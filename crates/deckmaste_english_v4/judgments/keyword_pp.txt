# "with"/"without" taking a keyword ability: a nominal modifier only
+PrepositionPhrase with flying => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of with
+PrepositionPhrase without flying => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of without
+PrepositionPhrase with first strike => KeywordPhrase "first strike" as Complement of with
+PrepositionPhrase with flying and haste => KeywordPhrase "flying and haste" as Complement of with
+ <card=Deluge> Tap all creatures without flying. => PrepositionPhrase "without flying" as Modifier of creature
+ <card=Moat> Creatures without flying can't attack. => PrepositionPhrase "without flying" as Modifier of creature
+ <card=Wallop> Destroy target blue or black creature with flying. => PrepositionPhrase "with flying" as Modifier of creature; !PrepositionPhrase "with flying" as Adjunct of destroy
# The keyword takes only its catalog-declared parameters: no free Preposition Phrase and no *where* Supplement
+ <card=Misty Salon> When you unlock this door, create an X/X blue Spirit creature token with flying, where X is the number of unlocked doors among Rooms you control. => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of with; PrepositionPhrase "where X is the number of unlocked doors among Rooms you control"; !KeywordPhrase "flying, where X is the number of unlocked doors among Rooms you control"; !PrepositionPhrase "of unlocked doors" as Modifier of flying
+ <card=Abuelo's Awakening> It's a 1/1 Spirit creature with flying in addition to its other types. => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of with; !KeywordPhrase "flying in addition to its other types"
+ <card=Rootha, Mastering the Moment> At the beginning of combat on your turn, if you've cast an instant or sorcery spell this turn, create an X/X blue and red Elemental creature token with flying and haste, where X is the greatest mana value among instant and sorcery spells you've cast this turn. => KeywordPhrase "flying and haste" as Complement of with; !KeywordPhrase "flying and haste, where X is the greatest mana value among instant and sorcery spells you've cast this turn"
