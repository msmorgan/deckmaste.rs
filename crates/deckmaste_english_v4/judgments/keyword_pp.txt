# "with"/"without" taking a keyword ability: a nominal modifier only
+PrepositionPhrase with flying => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of with
+PrepositionPhrase without flying => KeywordPhrase "flying" as Complement of without
+PrepositionPhrase with first strike => KeywordPhrase "first strike" as Complement of with
+PrepositionPhrase with flying and haste => KeywordPhrase "flying and haste" as Complement of with
+ <card=Deluge> Tap all creatures without flying. => PrepositionPhrase "without flying" as Modifier of creature
+ <card=Moat> Creatures without flying can't attack. => PrepositionPhrase "without flying" as Modifier of creature
+ <card=Wallop> Destroy target blue or black creature with flying. => PrepositionPhrase "with flying" as Modifier of creature; !PrepositionPhrase "with flying" as Adjunct of destroy
