# A bare relative clause opens with its Subject, never with a preposed adjunct: "Bare relatives always have the subject in initial position" (CGEL, Ch. 12 §3.5.5, p. 1055)
+ <card=Spark Double> You may have this creature enter as a copy of a creature or planeswalker you control, except it enters with an additional +1/+1 counter on it if it's a creature, it enters with an additional loyalty counter on it if it's a planeswalker, and it isn't legendary. => RelativeClause "you control" gap "creature or planeswalker"; !RelativeClause "if it's a creature, it enters"; !RelativeClause "if it's a creature, it enters with an additional loyalty counter on it"; !RelativeClause "if it's a creature, it enters with an additional loyalty counter on it if it's a planeswalker"
+ <card=Mirrorwood Treefolk> {2}{R}{W}: The next time damage would be dealt to this creature this turn, that damage is dealt to any target instead. => RelativeClause "damage would be dealt to this creature this turn" as Modifier of time gap "next time"; !RelativeClause "this turn, that damage is"
# An adverbial relative is bare too.
+ <card=Edgar, King of Figaro> Two-Headed Coin — The first time you flip one or more coins each turn, those coins come up heads and you win those flips. => NounPhrase "those coins" as Subject of come; !RelativeClause "each turn, those coins come up heads"
+ <card=Mindlink Mech> Whenever this Vehicle becomes crewed for the first time each turn, until end of turn, this Vehicle becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature that crewed it this turn, except it's 4/3, it's a Vehicle artifact in addition to its other types, and it has flying. => !RelativeClause "each turn, until end of turn, this Vehicle becomes a copy"; !RelativeClause "each turn, until end of turn, this Vehicle becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature that crewed it this turn"
