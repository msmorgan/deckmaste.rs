# The Targeting Marker is a determiner on its own, after an adjective, or the last word of a determiner with an outer one.
+NounPhrase target creature => DeterminerPhrase "target" head det:target as Determiner of creature; readings 1
+NounPhrase target player's graveyard => NounPhrase "target player" head player; DeterminerPhrase "target" head det:target as Determiner of player; readings 1
+NounPhrase other target creatures => DeterminerPhrase "other target" head det:target as Determiner of creature; AdjectivePhrase "other"; readings 1
+NounPhrase one or two target creatures => DeterminerPhrase "one or two target" as Determiner of creature; DeterminerPhrase "one or two"
# An adjective that needs an overt determiner is an ordinary premodifier, not a Targeting Marker.
+NounPhrase the only creature you control => AdjectivePhrase "only" head adj:only as Modifier of creature; DeterminerPhrase "the" as Determiner of creature; !DeterminerPhrase "only"; readings 1
# The Targeting Marker determines its nominal above every postmodifier; no postmodifier attaches above it.
+NounPhrase target creature you control => Nominal "creature you control"; DeterminerPhrase "target" as Determiner of creature; !Nominal "target creature"; readings 1
+NounPhrase up to one target artifact or enchantment that player controls => Nominal "artifact or enchantment that player controls"; !Nominal "target artifact or enchantment"
