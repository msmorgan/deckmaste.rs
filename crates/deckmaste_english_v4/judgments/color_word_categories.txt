# A colour word names the colour as a noun in a Quality (oracle-style-guide §10, "And, or, and and/or": *protection from black and from red*) and is the adjective after *be* or *become* (§12, "Colors and types"; *His tie is brown*, CGEL, Ch. 19 §3.3, p. 1642); an adjective without a noun conversion is a fused-head Quality (Ch. 5 §9.3, p. 417)
