# A colour word names the colour as a noun in a Quality (oracle-style-guide §10, "And, or, and and/or": *protection from black and from red*) and is the adjective after *be* or *become*, never also the noun (§12, "Colors and types"; *His tie is brown*, CGEL, Ch. 19 §3.3, p. 1642). An adjective without a noun conversion is a fused-head Quality (Ch. 5 §9.3, p. 417), the lexicon's `fused_quality`, and a colour word is not one: its Quality is one analysis, the noun (english-lexical-analysis §2)
