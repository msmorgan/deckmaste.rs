# Comma scope: a comma-set post-dependent runs to the end of its sentence, so a dependent after its head may contain one, and a dependent set off before its head by a comma, which ends at that comma, never does; the separating comma of a Clause coordination needs no closing mark, so either may contain one
# A comma-set Supplement may be inside or outside a later comma-set post-dependent.
+ <card=Endless Ranks of the Dead> At the beginning of your upkeep, create X 2/2 black Zombie creature tokens, where X is half the number of Zombies you control, rounded down. => Clause "X is half the number of Zombies you control, rounded down" || Clause "create X 2/2 black Zombie creature tokens, where X is half the number of Zombies you control"
# A sentence-final subordinate clause may contain a comma-set Supplement.
+ <card=Esper Sentinel> Whenever an opponent casts their first noncreature spell each turn, draw a card unless that player pays {X}, where X is this creature's power. => Clause "that player pays {X}, where X is this creature's power" as Complement of unless || Clause "draw a card unless that player pays {X}"
# The initial conjunct of a comma-delimited Clause coordination may have its own fronted dependent.
+ <card=Krark, the Thumbless> If you win the flip, copy that spell, and you may choose new targets for the copy. => Clause "If you win the flip, copy that spell" || Clause "copy that spell, and you may choose new targets for the copy"
+ <card=Disintegrate> If it's a creature, it can't be regenerated this turn, and if it would die this turn, exile it instead. => Clause "If it's a creature, it can't be regenerated this turn"; Clause "if it would die this turn, exile it instead" || Clause "it can't be regenerated this turn, and if it would die this turn, exile it instead"
# A preposed subordinate clause holds no comma-set post-dependent, in its own Clause, in a conjunct or in an Adjunct inside it: the intervening *if* follows the trigger condition (docs/oracle-style-guide.md §8, Intervening if).
+ <card=Stalwart Successor> Whenever one or more counters are put on a creature you control, if it's the first time counters have been put on that creature this turn, put a +1/+1 counter on that creature. => PrepositionPhrase "if it's the first time counters have been put on that creature this turn" as Adjunct of put; !Clause "one or more counters are put on a creature you control, if it's the first time counters have been put on that creature this turn"
+ <card=Gilded Cerodon> Whenever this creature attacks, if you control a Desert or there is a Desert card in your graveyard, target creature can't block this turn. => Clause "you control a Desert or there is a Desert card in your graveyard"; !Clause "this creature attacks, if you control a Desert"
+ <card=Wargling> Ferocious — Whenever this creature attacks while you control a creature with power 4 or greater, until end of turn, this creature gets +1/+0 and creatures you control gain trample. => !Clause "you control a creature with power 4 or greater, until end of turn"
# A middle conjunct of a serial Clause coordination may have its own fronted dependent, which cannot scope over the whole. No card has one; these entries are synthetic.
+ You draw a card, until end of turn, creatures you control get +1/+0, and you lose 1 life. => Clause "until end of turn, creatures you control get +1/+0"; Clause "You draw a card, until end of turn, creatures you control get +1/+0, and you lose 1 life"
+ Draw a card, if you control an artifact, draw a card, and you lose 1 life. => Clause "if you control an artifact, draw a card"
# A preposed subordinate clause may hold a comma-delimited Clause coordination: a separating comma between coordinates needs no closing mark, unlike the delimiting comma of a post-dependent (CGEL, Ch. 20, §3.1, pp. 1736–1737 [3]–[4]; §3.2.1, p. 1739; §3.2.5, p. 1745 [25]).
+ <card=Mishra, Claimed by Gix> If Mishra, Claimed by Gix and a creature named Phyrexian Dragon Engine are attacking, and you both own and control them, exile them. => Clause "Mishra, Claimed by Gix and a creature named Phyrexian Dragon Engine are attacking, and you both own and control them" as Complement of if
+ <card=Mishra, Claimed by Gix> If Mishra, Claimed by Gix and a creature named Phyrexian Dragon Engine are attacking, and you both own and control them, exile them, then meld them into Mishra, Lost to Phyrexia. => Clause "Mishra, Claimed by Gix and a creature named Phyrexian Dragon Engine are attacking, and you both own and control them" as Complement of if
