# Frequency adverbial noun phrases (`X times`, `two times`, `one time`) as adjuncts of the verb phrase
+NounPhrase X times
+NounPhrase two times
# from: Tourach's Gate
+NounPhrase one time
*NounPhrase one times
*NounPhrase two time
+VerbPhrase explores X times => NounPhrase "X times" as Adjunct of explore
+ When this creature enters, it explores X times. => NounPhrase "X times" as Adjunct of explore
# from: Nicanzil, Current Conductor
+VerbPhrase explores a card => NounPhrase "a card" as Object of explore
# from: Mnemonic Deluge
+VerbPhrase copy that card three times => NounPhrase "that card" as Object of copy; NounPhrase "three times" as Adjunct of copy
