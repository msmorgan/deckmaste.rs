**Audit and clear the remaining generated structural-recovery families.** Use
the grouped listing added by the prerequisite ticket as the complete worklist,
not merely the highest-frequency or most recently changed examples. For every
remaining exact-text family, establish with direct typed probes whether the
generated grammar already owns the necessary lexical items and constituent
constructions but lacks a composition, feature transition, admission rule, or
lowering path. Implement every such omission. Baseline recovery behavior is
evidence, not a compatibility contract, and must not be preserved merely
because older work or a workflow expected output to remain unchanged.

Retain structural recovery only where a causal probe demonstrates a genuinely
unsupported grammatical construction or lexical domain. Split those cases
into concrete follow-up tickets that record representative supported Oracle
text, the isolated failing constituent, the smallest missing capability, and
why an existing generated family cannot own it. Do not add card identities,
verbatim recovery leaves, new lexical opacity, manual surface-spelling
branches, parse costs, or dominance edges to force witnesses. Preserve every
tied holistic parse and test intended trees directly under normal, reversed,
and fixed-shuffle registration.

Completion is a causal grammar audit, not a lower recovery-count contest.
Every grouped family must have a disposition, and each implemented omission
must be proven by its generated owner, typed tree, source-independent exact
rendering, and registration neutrality. Review recovery and licensed-opacity
censuses for displacement or contamination, but never weaken or misclassify
neighboring syntax to improve them. The full source-independent round-trip,
ambiguity, performance, spelling-frame, Clippy, formatting, and citation gates
must pass. Commit no corpus rows or aggregate baseline snapshots. Standard
constraints apply.

The complete-worklist procedure is documented in
`docs/english-recovery-audit.md`. Export the unbounded runtime-derived JSON
before changing the grammar, annotate every exact group, and retain it outside
the repository. Final `--verify-worklist` verification must prove that every
implemented group disappeared, every retained group names its focused
follow-up, and no unaudited group entered the current corpus result.

## Resolution

Superseded by `english-construction-rewrite-design`. The preservation-heavy
construction architecture and exhaustive recovery-disposition approach are no
longer the direction of the project. This feature retains only the grammar
improvements and diagnostic experiments that are independently useful as an
interim snapshot or rewrite reference; it does not claim completion of the
original all-family worklist. No recovery baseline becomes a compatibility
contract, and no bulk family-ticket expansion is retained.
