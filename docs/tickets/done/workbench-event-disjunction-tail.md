## As landed (round of 2026-09-02)

Every count below was re-measured this round over `jq 'select(.supported)'`
against `data/derived/cards.jsonl`, with parenthesised reminder text

the corrected one is what to carry forward.

### Headline 1 — the general event disjunction: DECLINED, and the ask was
### aimed at the wrong function

The standing ask was to retire `AltEvent` for a `GameEvent` row, on the
theory that ~100 coordinated headers whose arms disagree are waiting on a
more general disjunction. They are not, and no count was needed to see it.

- The seat-versus-row question was already settled and stays settled:
  `eventName` is total, so a disjunction row would leave the classifier
  naming one of n events. That note sits above `GameEvent` and this round
  reused its reasoning for `Causes` (which lifts through it) rather than
  overturning it.
- What refuses those headers is `sharedCtx` and the words that read it,
  not the seat. `unionBindings` unions POINTWISE and has no union for
  announcement lists of different LENGTHS, so a coordination one of whose
  arms announces a participant the others do not falls back to the outer
  discourse bare. Moving the disjunction from a slot to a constructor
  changes not one step of that: the arms, their readers and `unionPayload`
  would be identical on the other shape. The move buys no card and costs
  three seats (`AltEvent`, `Delayed`, `Intercepts` all carry the arm list).
- **The live successor question, recorded at `sharedCtx` and not built:**
  the TAIL-ALIGNED union — align two announcement lists on their shared
  END instead of requiring equal length. It is index-safe as this
  vocabulary reads mentions (`It` counts with `countOnes`; the
  demonstratives match on word and payload), and it would move
  `badThreeArmHeaderReadback` — Giggling Skitterspike's "it", which every
  arm does announce. It is NOT built because it would also move
  `badAltHeaderMixedReadback` onto a referent the printed card does not
  mean: the bare-partner "blocks" arm announces only the self, so a
  tail-aligned union hands "that creature" the self rather than the
  blocker. Whether a union may name a determinate referent that is the
  WRONG one is the question to settle first, and it is a question about
  what those two pins claim — not a question about disjunction.

### Headline 2 — the periphrastic causer complex: its CORE landed, and the
### land-together pin's premise was falsified by re-measurement

The pin ("land TOGETHER or not at all — the parent round proved pieces buy
zero cards") rested on the complex being worth 3 "would CAUSE" lines. It is
worth **61**, and the payers divide cleanly.

**Re-measured.** The CAUSE verb outside reminder text is 76 supported
lines. Stripping the Clockwork ceiling and two singletons leaves one
construction with three tenses:

- **35 lines over 35 cards** write "that ability triggers an additional
  time" — the routed ADDITIONAL-TIME family, re-measured up from 32 lines
  / 35 cards. 20 of them put a causing event in front of the triggering
  ("if a land entering causes a triggered ability of a permanent you
  control to trigger"), 15 write the bare triggering.
- **23 lines over 22 cards** write the causation as a TRIGGER HEADER with
  a described source ("when a spell or ability an opponent controls causes
  you to discard this card").
- **3 lines** write it under "would" as a REPLACEMENT (Rain of Gore,
  Silhouette, Unpredictable Cyclone) — the ticket's own figure, confirmed.

**The premise the turn-structure round recorded for the additional-time
family was wrong on both halves.** It is not "a conditional replacement
over a triggering event with an ability-valued subject on `Intercepts`":
[CR#603.2d] gives the trigger multiplier its own procedure ("determine how
many times it should trigger, then that ability triggers that many times")
and states that such an effect "doesn't apply to other effects that affect
how many times an ability triggers" — the opposite of the reach [CR#614.5]
gives a replacement over modified events. So the cell is a replacement at
no point, and it is not the land/block/vote allowances' fourth sibling
either: an allowance raises a limit on what a player MAY do and this
effect gives no one a choice.

**Rows minted.**

- **`GameEvent.Triggers`** (the ability) — an ability TRIGGERING
  [CR#603.2], on `Activates`'s model and with its two gates. No actor
  slot, which is the one place it parts from `Activates`: [CR#113.3c] puts
  a triggered ability on the stack without anyone activating it. New
  `EventName.AbilityTrigger`, with rows in `sameEventName`,
  `eventHasMagnitude`, `lookbackSubjectOk` and `interceptOk`.
- **`GameEvent.Causes`** (cause, caused) — the periphrastic causation, a
  wrapper on `NthOccurrence`'s model. **`eventName` LIFTS THROUGH IT, and
  that is the whole event-composition answer** the trigger-3 round called
  blocking: [CR#603.2] matches a trigger condition against the event that
  OCCURRED, and the event that occurred is the caused one, so the
  causation is a restriction naming where it came from rather than a
  second event beside it. Every name-keyed table (`interceptOk`,
  `durationOk`, `triggerCountOk`) answers about the caused event and the
  classifier stays total. The cause is threaded BEFORE the caused event,
  which is what lets Rain of Gore's "its controller" and Psychic Purge's
  "that player" read the causer.
- **`Causing`** — the cause, three arms: `CausedBySource` (kind open on
  `IsDealtDamage`'s model; the printed causers are spells, abilities and
  the join), `CausedByEvent`, and the nullary `CausedByAnEffect`, which
  exists because [CR#609.1] makes an effect a happening and not an object
  there is any noun to describe (Library of Leng).
- **`StaticEffect.TriggersAdditionally`** (event, quantity) — the trigger
  multiplier [CR#603.2d]. The whole line is the row: "that ability
  triggers an additional time" states no content of its own, so there is
  no body slot. The event is gated by NAME (`triggerCountOk`), which is
  what lets `Causes` carry it. The count is a `Quantity` on
  `MayVoteAdditional`'s model with its three gates. New
  `StaticKind.TriggerMultiplier`.
- **`ControllerOf` widened** from `Noun bs Object` to any kind passing
  `controlKind` — the same test `ControlledBy` already asks. [CR#109.4]
  gives a controller to objects on the stack, so "its controller" over a
  spell-or-ability is the rules' own reading; 8 supported lines want it
  (6 are "counter that spell or ability unless its controller pays {N}").
  `OwnerOf` was NOT widened beside it and could not be: [CR#108.3]'s owner
  belongs to a CARD, and an ability on the stack is none.

**Benches — 4 whole cards.** `kataraTheFearless` (the bare triggering),
`nabanDeanOfIteration` (the causing event), `psychicPurge` (the described
source at a trigger header, reading "that player" back out of the causer),
`rainOfGore` (the causation at the replacement seat — the reason
`eventName` must lift through, since what [CR#614.1] replaces is the LIFE
GAIN).

**Pins — 2, in `ProofsG`.** `badTriggeringReplaced` (a replacement over an
ability's triggering; `interceptOk AbilityTrigger` is False) and
`badMultipliedNonTrigger` (a trigger multiplier over a death). Both were
checked non-vacuous by mis-stating them and confirming the build rejects
`Oh impossible`.

**Not landed, and why the pin no longer binds them together.** The
`Causer`-AS-NOUN widening (`Words.Causer`'s nullary `AnEffect` at
`CreationVoice`/`CausedBy`/`TokensCreated`/`CounterEvent`, plus
`badCausedCounterWithAgent`) is a DIFFERENT question from `Causing`: those
four rows record what a creation or a counter placement was DONE BY, and
none of the 61 landed lines reaches them. Its three payers each carry a
second blocker of their own — Zabaz needs "a modular triggered ability"
(Modular is absent from `keywordFacts`' 38 rows, and "the triggered
ability of keyword K" is a reading neither `AbilityHead` nor `AbilityOf`
has); Karmic Justice needs a described causer in `VerbedEvent`'s actor
seat, which is `Maybe (Noun bs Player)`; Cobra Trap needs the by-source
lookback agent. Crafty Cutpurse is one `TokenPhrase` arm (`Each p` beside
`CountedTokens`/`OneToken`) and stays unbuilt with them.
