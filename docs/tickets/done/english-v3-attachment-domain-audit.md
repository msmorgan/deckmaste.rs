and contrasting authentic Oracle constituents. Determine which derivations are
invalid, redundant or genuinely distinct before changing Admission or Reading

lexical bare-passive host. Preserve other grammatical By functions and independently

## Independent CGEL function audit

The following conclusions come from full-section consultation, not from preferred
Reading samples or a requested count. The original Oracle context was inspected
before identifying each constituent. A suffix beginning with *by* is not by that
fact a constituent: material continuing into a following clause is excluded.
In particular, an extracted suffix like “by exactly two Auras, it has ...” is
not an independently attested PP and supplies no attachment evidence.

Authentic contrasts:

- Avacyn, Guardian Angel: “Prevent all damage that would be dealt to another
  target creature this turn by sources of the color of your choice.” The
  intended *by* PP is an internalised Complement in the Bare Passive headed
  by *dealt*. The NP “sources of the color of your choice” is a complete
  constituent; its two Of attachment structures distinguish choosing the color
  from choosing sources of that color. Neither is an associative duplicate.
- Spikeshell Harrier: “If that opponent's speed is greater than each other
  player's speed, reduce that opponent's speed by 1.” The complete consequent
  VP has a scalar-change extent Complement. This is an active By use.
- Trusted Advisor: “Your maximum hand size is increased by two.” The By phrase
  expresses extent even though its host is passive. Bare Passive plus By does
  not therefore establish internalised Complement function.
- Noctis, Prince of Lucis: “You may cast artifact spells from your graveyard by
  paying 3 life in addition to paying their other costs.” The complete By phrase
  has a gerund-participial Complement and expresses means. This construction
  must remain available independently of passive complementation.

The exact grammatical findings are:

| Hypothesis | Finding and authority |
| --- | --- |
| Every By phrase is a passive internalised Complement. | Rejected. Means adjuncts occur in active and passive clauses and can coexist with an internalised Complement (CGEL Ch. 8 §2.2, pp. 673–675; Ch. 16 §10.1.1, p. 1428). Scalar-change By extent requires its appropriate lexical host (Ch. 8 §§5.2–5.3, pp. 691–693). |
| An internalised Complement can attach to any enclosing auxiliary as that auxiliary's passive participant. | Rejected for that function. Expanded passives contain a Bare Passive; the matrix modal is not itself passive (Ch. 16 §10.1.1, pp. 1427–1431, especially pp. 1430–1431). A different, adjunct interpretation of the same surface PP is a separate question. |
| Temporal adjunct NPs cannot contain postmodification. | Rejected. Temporal NPs permit dependent clauses and PPs (Ch. 8 §6.3, pp. 696–699, especially p. 698); nominal post-head PPs and stacked modification are independently general (Ch. 5 §14.2, pp. 444–447). This does not endorse Avacyn's temporal-NP By attachment as the intended interpretation. Its odd game interpretation alone does not prove syntactic invalidity. |
| Every intermediate auxiliary attachment is redundant. | Not established. Auxiliary-matrix and subordinate-clause adjuncts can have distinct scope; weak semantic contrasts do not remove their syntactic distinction (Ch. 8 §20.2, pp. 782–784). No universal flattening follows. |
| Any formal finite ellipsis path is licensed in the complete card. | Not established. Stranding can retain adjuncts and can occur at different auxiliary levels, but ordinarily requires a recoverable VP meaning in immediate linguistic context (Ch. 17 §7.1, pp. 1519–1523). The fragment's Omitted node does not establish that relation. |

Complement status cannot be inferred from optionality alone, and an unusual
semantic selection is not automatically ungrammatical (CGEL Ch. 4 §1.2,
pp. 219–228). Those distinctions prevent a word-level By ban, an agent-only
interpretation for every passive By phrase, and a temporal-noun shield.

No grammar or Reading-identity change is made on this audit evidence. In
particular, no member of the exploratory 184-reading set is certified valid
merely because a proposed blanket restriction failed, and no structural Reading
is merged on the strength of equal rendered text or equivalent game meaning.
