# Assess removing the protection retention wrapper

Parked by the user on 2026-09-06. `StaticSpec.retention` is a protection
attachment exception, not a general applicability wrapper. Its narrow use
does not by itself justify a new mechanism or immediate removal.

The proposed replacement is a protection-macro parameter qualifying only the
attachment consequences of one protection instance and the appropriate
excluded attachments. Preserve the already-attached selection at the relevant
time. It must not weaken damage, targeting, or blocking consequences, or
another protection instance. Benevolent Blessing and Black Ward are the
concrete witnesses.

Show a full expansion that preserves those scopes and is simpler than the
current wrapper before implementation. Leave retention in place during the
planned conditional, part-scope, and off-battlefield folds. Standard
constraints apply.
