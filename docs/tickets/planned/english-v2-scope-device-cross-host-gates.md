# Migrate coordination, grammatical selection and complete scope alternatives

The first shared interface is supplied by `english-v2-grammar-family-breadth`.
Complete the acceptance below against those interfaces; other families need
not finish their inventories before this work begins.

Implement [admission, selection and retained alternatives](../../english-grammar-design.md#admission-selection-and-retained-alternatives).
Retire structural-specificity arbitration and the exception path after all
candidates pass feature/dependency admission. Preserve the four declared
principles: qualification and distributive distributions, selected-role
preemption, and identity preference. Generalize declared-role/identity context
lifting over the migrated grammar. Equal bytes, construction counts and anchor
counts cannot create a preference or an equivalence edge.

Coordinate at the category that owns the bracketing: Nominal under a shared
Determiner, or complete NP with its own Determiner. Remove duplicate
locative/full-NP/zero-determiner wrappers for the same bracketing without
banning bare-plural coordination. Complete the existing and/or/and-or domain
for locative NPs and Keyword Quality. Model correlative coordination and but/nor
with declared Coordinator structure and explicit agreement/distribution;
extend the Lean witnesses before their production use.

Keep the generated hoisted AST and derived `AdmissibleSites`; add derived
anchorings and joint assignment rows to preserve correlated complete readings.
Neither fields nor dynamic frame rules introduce parsed alternatives, bytes,
leaves or specificity tiers. Expand/recheck the rows in diagnostics and compare
exact sets of complete readings. Preserve role opacity, first-eligible
preemption, lexical identity, coordinator identity, anchor topology and
unchanged wrappers. No field contains an alternative subtree.

Acceptance owns all scope rows in the register: Class C Adventure Awaits,
Class D Abzan Falconer, R7 wrapper overlap/Doorman and the recorded fourteen,
Vicious Rivalry, all thirteen with cases, the prior collapse positives and
negatives, and Arwen's two frame-pair bracketings. Assert exact reading sets,
representative paths, populated slots/anchor tuples, roundtrip and unchanged
lexical traversal. Four nested-mobile readings and their quotation embedding
must survive; flat n-ary versus nested binary must not pack. Sample the three
B7a gains with a second connective and at least twenty other B7a gains, keeping
their independently justified structures. Arwen's earliest anchor tuple is
presentation only, never a boundary preference.

Show an unrelated same-text homograph pair stays an ambiguity, and reverse
candidate enumeration without changing selection or classes. Do not reinterpret
a new non-scope tie as a pack. Report candidate/assignment counts and costs on
the witness set before attempting compression. Source evidence: style-guide
§10 “Logic, choice, and coordination”; the reviewed Lean selection laws bound the claims.

Standard constraints apply. Production correspondence and the applicable
[obligations](../../english-grammar-migration-obligations.md) are part of this
ticket; re-spell existing tests by their independently justified outcomes.
