# Recon: bound forms under existing lexical owners

Recon lexical-model item, routed by the orchestrator ruling of 2026-10-08 in
`english-v3-landwalk-stem-class-derivation`.

The preferred shape is a per-form binding on an existing lexeme: distinct
free/bound form identity under one owner, with form-specific binding and frame
lookup. Investigate how lexical indexing, admission and realization can retain
that distinction while preserving existing free forms and both roundtrip laws.

The current model declares binding and frames on the entire lexeme and rejects
duplicate inflection slots. The landwalk stem-class landing therefore retains
mechanically derived per-stem lexemes. This recon item does not authorize their
replacement; resolve the lexical-model shape and Reading identity migration
before proposing an implementation ticket. Standard constraints apply.
