# english-v4: *round* and noun *total* (F7, F9 of the oncology re-scan)

Two findings of the post-Stratum-7 oncology re-scan whose proposed fixes were
measured and do not hold. Each needs a way to say what the lexicon means, not a
witness-aimed exclusion.

1. **F7, *rounded down* as an active finite preterite.** `round` declares
   `Prepositional(["down","up"])`, so *X rounded down* reads as a finite clause
   and, in Aspect of Wolf, as a medial Subject remnant (16 -> 28 Readings).
   Dropping that frame (leaving `TransitiveWithPP`) removes the active reading
   but also the imperative *Round up each time.* (Peer into the Abyss,
   Hydroid Krasis), which then parses only as *round [each time] up* with the
   adverbial as the shifted object. That is a worse tree than the one it
   replaces. The imperative needs an intransitive-particle frame that has no
   finite use, or an object-omission analysis of *round up*; either is a
   frame-family or lexicon-schema capability to design.
2. **F9, *the total mana value of the cards exiled this way*.** The top Readings
   take noun *total* with a bare relative clause *[mana value of the cards]
   exiled this way*. The finding's two fixes: `Exclude $.Gap NP` on
   `MannerAdjunct` is not an equation (`Gap` is not a feature) and would also
   remove *the cards they exiled this way* (Memory Vessel, Rocco, and about 30
   other faces), whose Verb Phrase has the same NP Gap; and a required *of*
   Complement for *total* has no declaration (the bare `BareNominal` Valence
   takes every noun with no frame, and `obligatory` applies to a whole phrase,
   not one lexeme). The same bare relative reads *the number mana value of the
   cards exiled this way*, so the defect is general to relational nouns, not
   *total*. Design a lexeme-level obligatory Complement (a noun whose frames
   all demand one), then declare the relational nouns.

Standard constraints apply. No face lost.
