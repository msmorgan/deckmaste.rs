# *excess* must not be a bare nominal after *of* in *the amount of excess damage dealt this way*

Lacerate Flesh, Overwhelming Victory, Windswift Slice, Goblin Negotiation and
Hell to Pay read "... the amount of excess damage dealt [to that creature]
this way". After
[`english-v4-excess-verbless-clause`](../done/english-v4-excess-verbless-clause.md)
made `noun:excess` a count noun, it no longer heads a bare Noun Phrase, but
`of` still takes it as a bare singular count Nominal, the frame that serves
*end of turn* (`BarePrepositionalNominal`). The units keep wrong Readings in
which *amount of [excess]* is postmodified by a bare relative
*[damage dealt __ this way]*, separating *excess* from *damage*. The same
analysis existed before with *excess* as a bare mass Noun Phrase; only its
category changed.

## Change

- Find why a bare count Nominal after *of* is open to `noun:excess` (and to
  any count noun with an unfilled selected Complement) and remove the analysis
  structurally or lexically. No consumer-side exclude naming *excess*. *in
  excess of* and *the excess* (Toralf, God of Fury) must keep their intended
  analyses.

## Done when

- None of the five units has a Reading in which *excess* is separated from
  *damage*, covered by a `+` Judgment on real text.
- No face is lost, and no unit gains a Reading.

Standard constraints apply.
