# Stratum 3 residual: ten deferred units

The Stratum 3 landing read every Stratum 0–3 corpus unit except these ten,
which the owner deferred to this ticket. None needs a relative-clause mechanism;
each needs one of the following. Until all ten read, the census lists them as
regressions at the completed Stratum.

1. **Deictic temporal *this game*** (Commander's Insignia, Vanguard of the
   Restless, Jeska, Thrice Reborn, The Swarmlord: *for each time you've cast your
   commander from the command zone this game*). Declaring the noun *game*
   `Temporal` reads them but was rejected: it gives about 70 units such as *you
   win the game* and *begin the game* a second, wrong Reading (intransitive verb
   plus temporal adjunct *the game*). Find the distinction that makes *this
   game*, but not *the game* after *win*, a temporal adjunct (CGEL, Ch. 8 §6,
   p. 695, deictic temporal location), without a word-specific flag.
2. **Relative-clause extraposition** past a predicate adjunct (Skyship
   Weatherlight: *Choose a card at random that was exiled with Skyship
   Weatherlight*; Metzali, Tower of Triumph: *Choose a creature at random that
   attacked this turn*). The relative clause modifies the Object, not *random*;
   do not make *at random* a nominal postmodifier (it would double every
   *discard a card at random*).
3. **One-offs**, each a missing frame shape or nominal:
   - Ral Zarek: *for each coin that comes up heads* (*come up* with a
     predicative Complement, and nouns *heads*/*tails*).
   - Clay Champion: *for each {G}{G} spent to cast it* (*each* with a symbol
     sequence that takes a Postmodifier).
   - Deadly Complication: *You may have it become no longer suspected* (*no
     longer* inside the predicative after *become*; *is no longer suspected*
     already reads through the post-copular adverb).
   - Carth the Lion: *cost an additional [+1] to activate* (a loyalty bracket
     as an Object).

Standard constraints apply.
