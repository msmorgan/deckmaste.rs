# english-v4: bare Noun Phrase coordination at one level

Two bare Noun Phrases joined by a coordinator get two trees: a
coordination of Nominals under one bare Noun Phrase (BarePlural over
`Coordination:Nominal`) and a coordination of bare Noun Phrases. With no
determiner to share and no dependent shared by the Nominals, the two differ in
no meaning. Witnesses: *creatures and lands*, *damage and life*, *devotion to
[white and black]* (Master of Waves), *choose [black or red]*. Found measuring
`english-v4-colour-words-adjective-primary` (c); not colour-specific, so a
colour-only bypass of Nominal would be a witness-aimed fix.

Find which level CGEL gives a bare coordination (Ch. 15, Ch. 5 §8 on bare
NPs), keep the Nominal coordination where a shared determiner or dependent
needs it (*all creatures and lands*, *[creatures and lands] you control*),
and canonicalize structurally. Add it to english-lexical-analysis §2's list.

Also from that measurement, *it's both red and white* has 3 Readings: the
correlative coordination, *both* as a MeasuredComparative modifier of *red
and white*, and *'s [both]* with *red and white* as a depictive. Check whether
either of the last two is English.
