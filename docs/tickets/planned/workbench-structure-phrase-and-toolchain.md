# Settle the structure-vs-phrase verdicts and close the two toolchain gaps

One structure-vs-phrase sweep produced two piles: eight families it convicted as
phrase costumes to be extracted into macro vocabulary, and twelve more flagged as
possible twins plus two split-table tensions that it left unworked. Both piles
are read against the same lens, so they are one claimable unit. Riding with them
are the two items the sweep pushed out of the grammar entirely — the generated
lifted scans and a Rust-side emit arm. **Those last two are NOT grammar rounds**;
they are toolchain work, kept here because the sweep is what surfaced them.

## Extract the costume families into macro vocabulary

The workbench under-uses its function layer: it mints a fresh top-level
constructor per syntactic container, each wearing that container's phrase name,
where one utility node with a linearization rule exposed through the card
language would do. Under the settled direction that is exactly backwards — the
core is judged by lowerability and its algebra, never by surface affinity, and
common phrasings belong to the card language's functions.

Authority:
[The kind index joins; union marking is spelling](../../decisions/kind-index-joins-union-marking-is-spelling.md)
(the general rank rule, of which the union family was one instance) and
[Macros are declarative](../../decisions/macros-are-declarative.md) for where
those functions are headed. A structure-vs-phrase sweep worked sixteen families
to a verdict; the eight it convicted are below, with the evidence that convicted
them. Take those verdicts as given.

### The families the lens convicted

Work them in this order — the first three are the cheapest and the file's own
docstrings already admit the duplication:

1. **The ten `Not*` non-nesting witnesses** — nine of the ten are literally the
   same three lines and the docstrings admit it in a chain. Keep the aliases for
   pin legibility, unify the mechanism. Note two of the ten (`NotLetterRider`,
   `NotConditional`) are **not** self-nesting gates at all — they gate another
   row's body while wearing the family prefix.
2. **The letter machinery** — two rider rows quoting the **same spelling string**
   verbatim, the second deferring to the first for the nesting rule, plus a third
   letter binder that already exists with no constructor at all and 24 corpus
   lines reading it back. The read side is already unified and is the proof.
3. **`Not` / `NotCond`** on the two rows — the spelling comments are the same
   sentence twice. The three admission tables underneath are **genuinely three**
   and stay.
4. **`Compare` / `CompareAmt`** — the comparator and bound vocabularies are
   already shared whole and the register rule keys on the subject, not the
   frame; one subject slot absorbs the real difference.
5. **`ObjectCant` / `CantBe`** — same spelling string at both, same act catalog.
   What the second adds is *attachment*, not prohibition content, and the file
   has already built that decomposition next door.
6. **The prevention/redirection family** — the "relative clause vs event clause"
   argument fails the lens by these rows' own docstrings. What survives is a
   genuine amount-type difference; collapsing moves three measured facts out of
   "which row you picked" into three small tables.
7. **The four one-shot counter verbs** — a 2×2 grid of {add, remove} × {object,
   player scope} minted as four phrase-named rows, where the read side already
   took the lens's answer with one kind-indexed row spelling both surfaces.
8. **`Whose` / `Owner`** — a literal sub-enum, row for row, with the shared rows
   spelled identically; the docstring's own cited precedent is the counterexample
   (one vocabulary, N admission tables, which is the opposite of two enums). The
   ninety dead cells it names are a cost of indexing a table by the raw enum,
   answerable by a witness at the duration constructor.

**Out of scope, deliberately:** the families the sweep ruled genuinely distinct —
the skip/untap pairs (two rules, [CR#611.3b] against [CR#611.2a], with per-part
admission disagreeing in opposite directions over all 27 supported bare-skip
sentences); the intercept/replace/hold/delay/reflex five, each with its own
context threading; the status family, where the merge has already been performed;
and the four (part × owner) grids. Also out: the lifted scans, which are a
generation problem handled in the *Generate the lifted scans* section below and
not by the costume lens.

What resists in several verdicts is Idris and not English — `Effect` and
`StaticEffect` are distinct datatypes in one mutual block, and the five
telescopes exist for list-sugar name resolution. Where the defect is documentary
and table-level rather than constructor-count, fix the document and the table and
say so; do not force a merge Idris refuses.

## Work the flagged twin candidates and the split-table tensions to a verdict

The same sweep flagged twelve families as possible per-container twins — the same
semantic job re-minted once per syntactic container, each wearing that
container's phrase name — and worked **none** of them to a verdict. Beside them
sit two table splits whose only reason for existing is a disagreement on one
cell. Each item is one body comparison away from either a merge or a "leave it
alone" with the reason recorded; what is not worth having is the list sitting
unread while the macro-vocabulary work happens around it.

The lens to apply, and the house's own two postures: the **twin idiom** is named
at `OtherThan` — "the `Compare`/`CompareAmt` and `Not`/`NotCond` shape: one
namespace, two frames, and the argument in the name". The **utility idiom** is
named at the `GameEvent` gates, where `Interceptable`/`Holdable`/`Triggerable`/
`Awaitable` are four witness types over one `eventUse` table, existing only so a
pin says which question refused. The second is the recommended shape.

Authority:
[The kind index joins; union marking is spelling](../../decisions/kind-index-joins-union-marking-is-spelling.md)
— phrasing knowledge goes to the card language or the spelling declarations,
never to a core constructor.

### Do these two first

- **The five closed two-row `Nat` tables** — `NextUntapCount` (66/1),
  `ExtraTurnCount` (32-sentence/2), `SkipCount` (second cell = Eater of Days
  alone), `PhaseCount` (47/1, Full Throttle alone), `CapBound` (5/2). Five
  structurally identical tables, each explicitly ruled *not* shared with its
  siblings despite the identical cell shape, each second cell held by **one
  card**, and each carrying an explicit three-count pin (`badUntapNextThree`,
  `badThreeExtraTurns`, `badThreeAdditionalPhases`, `badUntapCapThree`). One
  `AttestedCount : (site) -> Nat -> Type` over one table carries all five
  measurements without losing a cell. A single "three extra turns" printing
  widens one and re-opens the not-shared ruling for all five, so this is also the
  cheapest live flip risk in the workbench.
- **The four (part × owner) grids** — `windowOk`, `headerWindowOk`, `partUse`,
  `spanUse`. These are **data, not constructions**, and a per-reader admission
  table over a shared vocabulary is the recommended shape already; the
  disagreements are measured and bidirectional (a second table exists because two
  readers disagree in both directions on six cells). The one real defect is that
  the index sets differ — `windowOk`/`headerWindowOk` index `TurnPart -> Maybe
  Owner` while `partUse`/`spanUse` index over `Whose`. Unify the vocabulary and
  the four become four readers of one grid.

### The two split-table tensions

- **`keywordCardOk`'s Flash cell.** `keywordStackRegime` says Flash is
  `AtCasting` per [CR#113.6e]; the printing table says 598/598 permanent-only, 0
  spell (`badFlashOnInstant`, "the word would be vacuous there"). **The two-table
  split exists purely because they disagree on this one keyword** — one printed
  instant carrying Flash collapses the reason for two tables, and the same shape
  recurs at `windowOk`/`headerWindowOk`'s six-cell disagreement above. Decide
  whether a merged table (already declined once), a third table, or one
  vocabulary with a witness at the disagreeing row is right, and record which.
- **`grantableAb`'s three quotation cells.** Three cells flipped when quotation
  was re-read as a *spelling* rather than a construction, on a 763-vs-0
  (non-keyword grants always quoted) and thousands-vs-0 (nullary keyword grants
  never quoted) split, with exactly one crossing class — the parameterized
  keywords — resolved by the parameter itself (Ward bare 49, all mana-cost;
  quoted 4, all em-dash). The re-reading is a whole chapter's conclusion resting
  on cells, and a single bare quoted-flying line or a crossing Ward line unpicks
  it. Under the settled direction the conclusion is right in kind — quotation is
  spelling — so record it where spelling facts live rather than leaving it as an
  inference over three cells.

## Routed ledger items

Items from closed round tickets that this ticket owns. One line each, citing
the done ticket that recorded them.

- **`Aggregate` is now a special case of `AggregateOver`.** `Aggregate op ax p`
  folds `ax` over the members of `p`; `AggregateOver op p body` folds `body`
  over the same members with one bound, so writing the axis read as the body
  recovers the older row exactly. Two spellings for one meaning; a future round
  should collapse the pair rather than grow both —
  `docs/tickets/done/workbench-amount-comparison-and-quantity.md`.
- **`writtenBound` has no consumer** anywhere in the repo — signature and rows
  only, likely residue of a removed bound gate; the merge of `XVal = True` with
  `DefinedLetter = False` is inert. Conductor decides deletion —
  `docs/tickets/done/workbench-amount-comparison-and-quantity.md`,
  `docs/tickets/done/workbench-letters-introduce-then-define.md`.
- **`grantableAb (Spell _) = False` is narrower than [CR#113.3a]** for one
  subject: `Gains` takes a `Noun bs Object`, which can name a spell. 0 corpus
  lines grant a quoted ability to a spell, so the cell refuses nothing printed,
  and the docstring's argument holds for a permanent subject only. Belongs with
  the three quotation cells above —
  `docs/tickets/done/workbench-closure-flip-risks.md`.
- **The `Rider`-family unification stays deferred**, per the 2026-08-24
  decision: it waits on a second trailing-rider construction existing —
  `docs/tickets/done/workbench-one-define-spelling.md`.

## Acceptance

- Each convicted family lands as one node plus a linearization rule plus the
  card-language functions that keep the phrase names, or is recorded as refused
  with the Idris reason.
- Every measurement a collapsed row was carrying survives as a table cell.
- Every flagged twin candidate and split-table tension above has a recorded
  verdict; "already the good shape" is a valid and expected outcome.
- Any merge preserves every measured cell, including the dead ones — a cell
  dropped in a merge is a silent widening.
- The generated definitions are byte-for-byte what a reviewer would have written
  by hand, and the generator is the only place the shape is stated.
- Totality and coverage are preserved: a new constructor in any scanned datatype
  produces a generation error or a new arm, never a silently missing case.
- The `OneOrMore` gap error is gone and a filter of this shape emits.
- A regression case covering the counted-group / universal distinction exists.
- `idris/scripts/build` PASS, no witness lost, no pin silently passing.
