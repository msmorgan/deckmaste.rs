# Compose postpositive comparative Determinative Phrases after measured NPs

## Why

The adjective-complement landing reconciles *equal*, *less*, *greater* and
*other* with their selected Complements. It does not recategorize determinative
*more* as an Adjective. CGEL, Ch. 5 §14.2(a), p. 445 explicitly describes
post-head comparative Determinatives with a Determiner, including an expanded
*less than* phrase. Their postpositive function is distinct from the
DP-internal cardinal comparisons completed in
`english-v3-comparative-quantity-determiners`.

On refreshed base `mupwkuzv` (18,019 covered), eight supported faces contain
*life more than*, all unread: Angel of Destiny, Chalice of Life, Doctor Strange,
Surgeon, Righteous Valkyrie, Ajani, Strength of the Pride, Leyline of Hope,
Speaker of the Heavens, and Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim. Evidence is in the
comparative-complement landing's ignored `target/english-v3/` directory.
These are overlapping host and dependent gaps, not a gain forecast. Cosmos
Elixir also remains unread because the nominal constituent *your life total*
is missing, although its *greater than* adjective comparison is now supplied.
Include that nominal host as a witness when reconciling *life total*.

## Goal

Read the comparative DP in authentic constituents such as Chalice of Life's
*at least 10 life more than your starting life total*, retaining its
Determinative lexical head, bare nominal Complement, selected *than*, and
postpositive function. Reconcile the measured-NP host and *life total* noun
composition if still missing; preserve the existing cardinal DP analyses.

## Witnesses

- Chalice of Life: "{T}: You gain 1 life. Then if you have at least 10 life more
  than your starting life total, transform this artifact."
- Righteous Valkyrie: "As long as you have at least 7 life more than your
  starting life total, creatures you control get +2/+2."
- Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim: "{1}{W}{B}, Sacrifice another creature: Exile target
  nonland permanent. Activate only if you have at least 10 life more than your
  starting life total."

## Method

Standard constraints apply. Reuse the existing comparative Complement
interface where its Category is appropriate; do not add an Adjective homograph
for *more*. Prove non-overlap with existing *more than one* quantity Readings
and preserve per-face Reading counts against the refreshed base.
