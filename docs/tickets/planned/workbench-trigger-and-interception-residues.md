# Close the trigger header's holes and pay the interception's owed bench

One unit covering everything the trigger header and the replacement/interception
side still owe: the header's missing heads, windows and defenders; the
header-internal "while", the two-header join and the coordinated readback; the
flip verb's event and condition halves plus the standing trigger shape; the
entry interception's fifteen unbenched lines; and the status change's admission
as a replacement antecedent. They are one claimable unit because they all move
the trigger header, `eventUse` and the `Intercepts` frame together — the same
event table decides which cells are trigger-only and which are interceptable,
and several residues below are each other's blockers (the "while" clause is
Preacher of the Schism's and Veiling Oddity's; the status-change cell and the
`Intercepts` window are one card's two halves).

## The BARE CARD HEAD

No `Predicate` heads a phrase with the word "card" on its own (finding 561). The
card it costs is STONEBINDER'S FAMILIAR — "Whenever one or more cards are put
into exile during your turn" — whose window and whose once-per-turn limit both
already write, so it is blocked on this alone.

The zone clause is not a workaround: `InZone` heads with the word because
[CR#109.2] names a zone and the word "card" in one breath, and Rakshasa Vizier
could carry the source's zone unspelled only because its event WROTE one. This
card writes no source at all. What is wanted is either a bare card head or a
reading that lets the DESTINATION license the word.

Measured carriers: Stonebinder's Familiar, Ennis, Ketramose, Laelia, The War
Doctor, Dutiful Knowledge Seeker, Wan Shi Tong, plus the 10 "one or more cards"
and 6 "a card" put-into subjects counted alongside them.

## The header windows with no `TurnPart` row — eight lines

- **"During your main phase"** 3 (Dovin's Acuity, Canyon Vaulter, Reckless
  Velocitaur). NOT a gap any more: `TurnPart.MainPhase` was minted on
  [CR#505.1]'s own sentence (the two main phases "are individually and
  collectively known as the main phase") and `headerWindowOk MainPhase (Just
  Yours)` is True, so all three lines write. **The pin
  `badHeaderMainPhaseWindow` IS NOT RETIRED**: it refuses the SPECIFIC row,
  which is still the wrong row for a card that does not say which phase, and
  its docstring's argument is what the new row vindicates. Do not retire it.
  Landed with the row: `EachYours` triggers on it (Carpet of Flowers, Frontier
  Siege).
- **The six delayed headers over the main phase** are `PartUnclaimed` — "at the
  beginning of your next main phase" (Mana Drain, Mana Sculpt, Scattering
  Stroke, Pygmy Hippo, Conduit of Storms twice) and "at the beginning of the
  next main phase this turn" (Vivien's Stampede). All six wait on the ORDINAL,
  not on the part; a residue of its own and not fixed by a `TurnPart` row.
- **"During the declare attackers step"** 4 (Misleading Signpost, Portal Mage,
  Portal Manipulator, Windshaper Planetar) wants a `TurnPart` row that does not
  exist.
- **"During each of their turns"** 1 wants a possessor `Owner` does not have.

Each is one row; none has a whole card behind it yet.

## The header-internal "while" clause — 60 lines

60 header-internal lines, **none comma-marked**, attaching the clause to the
EVENT rather than sitting between two commas after it. [CR#603.4] explicitly
does not reach it ("the word 'if' has only its normal English meaning anywhere
else"), so this is **not** a marking variant of the intervening slot.

At least twelve condition shapes, and three of them name an action IN PROGRESS
rather than a state — "while you're activating a craft ability", "while casting
a spell with emerge", "while scrying" — which is why it cannot be a marking word
on a state condition and needs its own design. The saddle family alone is about
twenty-five of the sixty.

Named payoff: **Veiling Oddity**, whose body, zone check and intervening slot
all elaborate — the last-removal event's only reachable bench line. Its second
blocker has moved: the self-PRONOUN half is fixed (`It` resolves off the event
subject) and what remains beside this word is the self's ZONE EVIDENCE — the
sorted self projects the battlefield by [CR#109.2] and the card asks about exile
(findings 346 and 351).

## The "and whenever" two-header join — 58 lines

58 supported lines, and **not** the landed event coordination. "When this
creature enters and whenever it attacks while saddled, create a 3/3 green
Elephant creature token" (Autarch Mammoth) writes TWO trigger words and joins
two whole headers over one effect, where `AltEvent` coordinates two events under
one word.

The measured difference is exactly the word: "Whenever X or Y" **never** writes
a second header word, and "When X and whenever Y" **always** does. So the two
families are told apart by the surface, and this one wants a second slot or a
second row. Cards: Autarch Mammoth, Brinelin, Case File Auditor, Balemurk Leech,
MACH-1, Exuberant Fuseling, Cryptid Inspector, Inspired Skypainter and fifty
more; several are otherwise-writable whole cards.

## The coordinated header's readback — 144 of 364

The tail the coordination round measured and did not pay: 144 of the 364
coordinated headers read a mention back, and `headerCtx` answers the empty
discourse for every coordination, so all 144 stay unwritable.

The reason is honest — the two disjuncts announce different things and the
sentence gives no way to say which happened — but it is not the whole truth for
one pair: the **46** "blocks or becomes blocked" lines announce the SAME phrase
on both sides, `eventAfter (Blocks n (Just m))` and `eventAfter (BecomesBlocked
n (Just m))` both being `nomIntro m`, and "that creature becomes green" (Aisling
Leprechaun) reads it. What reaches them is a COMMON ANNOUNCEMENT — the two
after-discourses where they agree — which needs a decidable comparison on
`Bindings` this vocabulary does not have. **Start there, not at the carrier,
which is already right.**

## The ENTRY interception's owed bench — 15 supported lines, 0 benched

The entry event was corrected to `InterceptedAndTriggered` and the composition
probed green, but not one of its fifteen supported lines benched — each
sub-family carries its own blocker, and they are small, adjacent clause-level
gaps. `eventUse Entry` is `InterceptedAndTriggered` on [CR#614.1d]; the
composition is green. The blockers:

**(a) The land cycle — 9 cards plus Mox Diamond.** "If this land would enter,
sacrifice a Forest instead. If you do, put this land onto the battlefield. If
you don't, put it into its owner's graveyard." Two conditionals over ONE
antecedent: `Reflexively` writes "if you do" and NOTHING writes "if you don't".
That pair is the whole ask. Every land in the cycle also needs mana production,
which has landed.

SHELTERED VALLEY was recorded as the cycle's exception and cheapest target —
"instead sacrifice … , then put this land onto the battlefield", no conditional
pair at all — on the claim that mana production alone stood between it and the
bench. **That is CORRECTED**: its mana line writes today and the card still
refuses, because "sacrifice each other permanent named Sheltered Valley you
control" wants a SACRIFICE clause (no `Effect` row spells one; The Golden Throne
is blocked on the same thing at a cost position) and a name-matching predicate
besides. Do not schedule Sheltered Valley as the cheap one.

**(b) The cast-history conjunct — 4 cards** (Containment Priest, Hallowed
Moonlight, Mistcaller, Primeval Spawn): "and it wasn't cast", agentless, over a
permanent that is entering, where `CastBy` demands a possessor and seeds the
stack. It is the same passive the cast-relation entry ledgers from the
description side (Errant, Street Artist). Hallowed Moonlight is a two-line
instant the moment it lands.

**(c) Two singletons.** Gather Specimens wants an ENTRY as a replacement BODY
("it enters under your control instead"), which no `Effect` row writes. Don't
Blink wants a source zone on the entry event plus a disjunction over it.
