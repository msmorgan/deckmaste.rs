# Close the counter region: placement interception, kind-blind events, bound reads and the kind catalog

Everything the counter region still owes, as one claimable unit: the
counter-scaling replacement and the entry-counter row's leftover carriers, the
bound read of a counter-bearing description with the kind-blind quantifiers and
the catalog's tail, and the kind-blind counter EVENT with the two waiting counter
kinds behind it. They are one round because they sit on the same declarations —
`CounterEvent`, `CounterBatch`, `CounterKind`, `HasCounters`, `EntersWithCounters`
and the counter-kind words — and each one's decision moves a slot the others read.

## Intercepting counter placement, and the entry-counter row's residues

The counter-scaling replacement has its scale vocabulary already built and is
missing only a carrier. Beside it sit the entry-counter row's leftover carriers
and a ledger of cards each blocked on one named other line.

### The counter-scaling replacement — 17 sentences / 21 occurrences

"If one or more [X] counters would be put on [subject], that many plus one (14)
/ twice that many (6) are put on it instead": Hardened Scales, Winding
Constrictor, CONCLAVE MENTOR (this family, NOT the entry-counter one), Doubling
Season, Corpsejack Menace. Measured and deliberately not built, with the
transferable half named:

- `DamageScale` already carries `Multiplied` and `Shifted ShiftUp`, exactly the
  two values this family writes and no third — the SCALE VOCABULARY is ready.
- The carrier is not. `Scales` is keyed by `DamageKind` and takes a damage
  source, where this family intercepts a counter placement on a described
  permanent, which is `Intercepts`' shape.
- Hardened Scales ({G}, one line) and Corpsejack Menace are the cheapest wholes.
- Its union-headed neighbours (Vorinclex, Innkeeper's Talent, Lae'zel) are NOT
  this family's — they belong to the distributive counter-kind anaphor (see *The
  kind-blind event and the distributive counter-kind anaphor* below), whose
  recipient read is already cleared.

### The entry-counter row's other two carriers

The COPY EXCEPTION (4) and the GRANTED QUOTATION (1) — two carriers of the same
clause, neither of them `EntersWithCounters`'s.

## A counter read on a comparison's left, kind-blind quantification, and the kind catalog's tail

Three counter-cluster leftovers sit on the same declarations: the bound read of a
counter-bearing description, the two families that want a quantity over counter
KINDS, and the catalog rows that wait only on a landable line.

Authority: [The kind index joins; union marking is spelling](../../decisions/kind-index-joins-union-marking-is-spelling.md)
— if the player-scoped bound read is taken, take it at the kind index; do not
mint a marked player-side twin of an Object-fixed row.

### The counter count as a bound — 11 lines over two scopes

Five OBJECT-scope frames bound the number instead of testing existence:
"Creatures you control with three or more +1/+1 counters on them have haste"
(RUNADI, BEHEMOTH CALLER; CHAMPION'S DRAKE, NILS DISCIPLINE ENFORCER, SHADOW
URCHIN, SPARK RUPTURE). Six PLAYER-scope frames are the corrupted keyword's, and
they are the WHOLE of the player-scoped counter description: "each opponent who
has three or more poison counters" (FEED THE INFECTION, GETH'S SUMMONS, GLISSA'S
RETRIEVER, IXHEL SCION OF ATRAXA, PHYREXIAN ATLAS, WURMQUAKE).

- `HasCounters` is fixed at Object because the bare player existence it could
  have spelled is ZERO lines, and [CR#122.1f] states the player-side test in the
  ranged shape too ("a player is 'poisoned' if they have one or more poison
  counters"). Keep that zero visible in whatever this round decides.
- ONE BLOCKER, named: `Predicate.Compare` bounds a `Characteristic`, whose whole
  vocabulary is Power, Toughness and ManaValue, so a counter READ cannot stand on
  a comparison's left. `CountersOn` already exists as the amount; the missing
  piece is the comparison that takes one. The operand-side twin (`XVal` on a
  comparison's left, 4 lines) is queued in the comparison round and this is its
  description-side half — probably one round together.
- DO NOT give `HasCounters` a quantity slot: that duplicates `Comparator` for
  eleven lines, and was deliberately not reached.

### Kind-blind counter quantifiers, and moving a counter

Two families want one thing the `Amount` vocabulary has no term for, a quantity
over KINDS rather than over a number:

- Bare "a counter" — 82 lines.
- "Move a counter from … to …" — 36 lines, which additionally wants a two-holder
  transfer verb.

Closed, and not to be re-proposed as a kind-quantifier: the presence read "with a
counter on it" is `HasCounters Nothing` (46 frames, family 221) and needs no
kind-quantifier at all — the `Maybe` says whether the sentence NAMES a kind, not
how many kinds it means. The always-all player removal is likewise not here: it
is `LosesAllCounters`' unnamed cell, that verb taking no number.

### The catalog's remaining tail

Minting doctrine, two tests: the corpus must write the kind as a one-shot put or
remove, and this grammar must be able to write that line. Eight kinds clear the
FIRST test and wait only on a landable line — oil (28 puts / 29 removes), quest
(33/10), level, spore, storage, ki, verse, depletion. Each enters the catalog on
the round that benches one of its lines; none needs a design decision first.

- `Lore` and `Age` are MINTED. Do not re-file either as cross-linked out.
- ONE kind stays cross-linked out: energy (the symbol family).
- Loyalty is [CR#122.1e]'s own mechanic and is not a flat kind. Its own measured
  refusal and its price are in *The loyalty counter kind* below.
- **Shield is NOT the same case, despite `CounterKind`'s comment saying so.** A
  later measurement refutes it: shield is an ordinary waiting kind beside the
  eight above. The correction and the comment fix are in *Shield counters* below;
  do not re-derive the refuted claim here.

## Making the counter event kind-blind, and the two waiting counter kinds

The counter event's required kind blocks a whole anaphor family, and two counter
kinds sit behind it — one refused on a sharpened reason with a measured price,
one refused by a comment the count refutes. Same table.

Authority: [The kind index joins; union marking is spelling](../../decisions/kind-index-joins-union-marking-is-spelling.md)
— Chandra, Fire Artisan's "target opponent or planeswalker" is a cross-kind
mention and not this work; take the counter kind alone.

### The kind-blind event and the distributive counter-kind anaphor — 10 lines, one thing

"That many plus one of each of THOSE KINDS of counters" is 10 supported lines
(Doc Samson, Innkeeper's Talent, Lae'zel, Loading Zone, Pir, Vorinclex ×2,
Winding Constrictor ×2, and Aragorn at the trigger position), and every one of
them PRESUPPOSES a kind-blind announcement: "each of those kinds" can only range
over a batch that may hold several kinds, which is exactly what `CounterEvent`'s
required `kind` refuses. So the anaphor cannot land before the kind-blind event
and **the two are one round** — finding 285's territory, with Stalwart
Successor's header the trigger-side carrier.

Doubling Season's second line is the marquee behind it: its causer half composes
green and the required kind is the only refusal left, so the card goes whole the
moment this lands. Four of the ten lines (Vorinclex ×2, Innkeeper's Talent,
Lae'zel) put their counters on "that permanent or player", and **that read is
landed** (`NounWord.JoinW`), so what is left on those four is this entry's own
kind-blind event and nothing else.

**Not this family, recorded so it is not swept in:** "those counters" is 16
lines, but 15 of them move or remove a set of counters already ON an object ("if
it had counters on it, put those counters on target creature" — The Ozolith,
Iron Apprentice, Resourceful Defense and four more): a different anaphor over a
different thing, and its own entry when someone wants it. Beside it, "counter of
that kind" is 14 lines of a CHOSEN kind ("choose a counter on target permanent …
put a counter of that kind on"), a chosen-quality axis and a third family again.

### The loyalty counter kind — 62 lines, and the reason it is refused

Recorded whole so the chain is not re-derived. Bioessence Hydra's second line
("Whenever one or more loyalty counters are put on planeswalkers you control,
put that many +1/+1 counters on this creature") needed three things. The batch
size is paid (`ThatMuch` over `OutcomeSort.CountersPut`) and the holder is no
longer missing — `Planeswalker` is a `CardType` row and two planeswalkers are
benched whole. What is left of the chain is **the counter kind alone**.

Finding 567 refused it on a sharpened reason that still holds: loyalty counters
are a CHARACTERISTIC's current value ([CR#122.1e,306.5c,109.3]) where stun,
poison and rad are markers. The price of that refusal is measured: **62**
supported lines write a loyalty counter, and two of the five Talents are blocked
on nothing else (Vivien's "put a loyalty counter on enchanted planeswalker",
Teferi's the same). Bioessence Hydra's FIRST line wants loyalty as a readable
count as well. Chandra, Fire Artisan is the same chain plus a cross-kind
disjunction over the type word, which is out of scope here. Whichever round
revisits this is revisiting a measured question, not a remembered one.

### Shield counters — an ordinary waiting kind, and a comment to correct

Finding 567 found `CounterKind`'s comment claiming shield "clears neither" half
of finding 85's test, and the count refutes it: **14** supported lines write a
one-shot put ("Put a shield counter on target creature", Boon of Safety; Brokers
Veteran; Aegis of the Legion) and **one** writes a remove. Shield is therefore
an ordinary WAITING kind beside oil, quest, level, spore, storage, ki, verse and
depletion — a row the moment a line writing it lands — and not a refusal.
[CR#122.1c]'s replacement-and-prevention pair is machinery the engine owns,
exactly as stun's is.

## The closure grid this family owns

Ranked below the workbench's fifteen top flip risks — the grid's many zeros are
independent measurements, so a printing moves one cell and the closure survives —
but every cell this family moves is one of them. A widening names the cell it
moved and re-reads the grid rather than defaulting it, and a cell left closed
says whether a rule or a count is closing it. Rows, evidence and widening costs:
[the closure tables](../../idris-workbench-closure-tables.md) §2.3.

## Acceptance

- The counter-scaling replacement lands on `Intercepts`' shape, reusing
  `Multiplied` and `Shifted ShiftUp` — no third scale value is minted, and the
  14/6 split is what justifies that.
- The union-headed neighbours stay out of the counter-scaling family.
- The copy exception and the granted quotation are carried by their own
  carriers, not folded into `EntersWithCounters`.
- Hardened Scales and Corpsejack Menace bench whole.
- The bound read lands as a comparison that takes a counter amount, not as a
  quantity slot on `HasCounters`.
- The player-side zero for bare existence survives as a pin or as a recorded
  measurement; it does not become a silent yes.
- Kind-blind quantification is decided for the bare-"a counter" and move
  families only; the presence read and the always-all removal stay out.
- Any catalog row added is paid for by a benched line writing that kind as a
  one-shot put or remove; energy stays out.
- `CounterEvent` admits a kind-blind batch and the distributive anaphor reads it;
  the two land together, Doubling Season's second line goes whole, and Stalwart
  Successor's header carries the trigger side.
- The two neighbouring families ("those counters" 16 lines / 15 non-family,
  "counter of that kind" 14 lines) are not swept in.
- Shield's `CounterKind` comment is corrected against the 14 put / 1 remove
  count, and shield is listed among the waiting kinds.
- Loyalty is either minted against finding 567's reason with the 62-line price
  stated, or left refused with that reason restated — not left implied.
- `idris/scripts/build` PASS, no witness lost, no pin silently passing.
