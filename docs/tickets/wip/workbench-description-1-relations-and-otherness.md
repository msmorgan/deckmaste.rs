# description-1: the relations, otherness and possessor rows

Sub-round 1 of [workbench-description-and-player-sorted-reads](workbench-description-and-player-sorted-reads.md)
(the umbrella — authoritative for measurements, pins and acceptance). Owns its
sections "Otherness complement residues", "The control tests the description
rows refuse", "Ownership as a possessor axis, and the plural relational
possessor", and "The ownership predicate and the per-game history reads",
plus these routed items: the Adventure-face re-measure; the commander
description ("Commander creatures you own/control"); the `negatable
(QualityNoun _)` over-reach; the all-graveyards possessor; the attacked-by
predicate (kind-polymorphic `Object \/ Player` relatum); the bare CARD word +
the permanent-card class + `castComplementOk (Just Battlefield)` re-ground;
`seedTy (HasSubtype s)` (Rukarumel); the characteristic LIST on `Compare`
(Talion); the object partitive over a described group + "all X from among
them"; and the plural possessor gates (`OwnerOf`/`ControllerOf` at `OneOf` —
Don't Blink) with the missing `Owner` values (Freyalise's Winds, Valgavoth).

Key pins (umbrella authoritative): the distributive mill is COUNTED before
any row; `Other`'s targeting refusal unchanged; the definite's announcement
survives into the condition frame — never re-routed through `Exists`; ONE
`OwnedBy` predicate serves description and axis; the meld blocker is stated
(a non-melding ownership carrier benched, or the round records it lands no
card); the third reader (The Fallen) is its own shape, not a copy; the
keyword-action event name answers cycling and the surveilled per-turn read
TOGETHER; the two per-game zeros survive in writing; Consume's benched
singular does not regress. Standard constraints apply.

## As landed (2026-08-28)

Every count below is this round's own re-measurement, over supported cards
with parenthesised reminder text stripped — the convention that reproduces
finding 111's 104/102 exactly.

### Otherness complement residues

- **The subset complement** — 104 occurrences over 102 cards, confirming
  finding 111. `ObjectP` now carries the mention's own **cardinality**, the
  fact no binding recorded: the library slice reads it off a literal
  amount, the counted and targeted determiners off an exact quantity, a
  per-member pass writes one, and `sized` writes it onto a binding
  `bindFor` already built. `TheOther` is `TheRest`'s singular twin, gated
  on `theOtherOk` — one counted group, and parts taken totalling all but
  one of its members. Benches **Codecracker Hound**. Both sides of the
  gate are measured in the bench (`theOtherAfterATwoCardLook`,
  `theOtherNeedsAStatedCount`, `theRestStandsWhereTheOtherRefuses`): a
  group whose size the text never stated keeps "the rest".
- **The distributive mill** — **62 supported lines**, not the one card the
  umbrella had priced (Altar of the Brood, Ruin Crab, Fractured Sanity,
  Syr Konrad and 58 more). Counted before anything was built, and then
  unblocked for free by the distributive-agent fix below. Benches **Altar
  of the Brood**.
- **"Another of those"** — re-measures at **1 occurrence over 1 card**
  (Clockspinning), not 4. Recorded with `SomeOf`'s family and still under
  finding 85's bar on its own; the partitive-base split below did not
  reach it, because the group it reads is a COUNTER mention rather than an
  object one.
- **The Grandeur discard** — re-measures at **7 cards**, not 12 (Baru,
  Korlash, Linessa, Oriss, Page, Skoa, Tarox). A cost-line question:
  the complement composes today, and what the seven want is the discard
  cost around it. Not otherness, and not this round's.
- **`Other`'s targeting refusal is unchanged.**

### The control tests the description rows refused

- **The definite under a control test** — 6 supported lines, confirmed
  (Abzan Beastmaster and Thickest in the Thicket on toughness/power,
  Padeem on mana value, Summon: Fenrir, Triumph of Cruelty, Triumph of
  Ferocity). `Matches` now takes a `TestSubject` instead of a
  `Bindingless` subject, and `condDelta (Matches n _) = nounDelta n ++
  selfSubjDelta n` — so the definite's announcement **survives into the
  governed clause** rather than being dropped at the condition's door.
  Nothing is re-routed through `Exists`. Benches **Padeem, Consul of
  Innovation**. `TestSubject` is a Bool gate and not a two-constructor
  witness, so `badMatchesTargetSubject` still refutes the whole gate at
  once (its witness moves from `Refl` to `Oh`).
- **`That` under an `Each` agent** — the fix is the one the bench's own
  `eachPlayerBindsNoSingular` measurement named: `agentIntro` lifts
  `AggregateOver`'s element binder from the AMOUNT sort to the EFFECT
  sort. `Does` types its body in `agentIntro subj`, and an `Each` subject
  hands the clause ONE member bound `TheD OneOf` **in place of** the group
  mention (beside it would leave "a creature of their choice" with two
  players to attribute the choice to). `Each`'s own delta is untouched, so
  `eachPlayerBindsAGroup` still holds and "those players" still reads the
  set. Benches **Crackling Doom** (the demonstrative), **Soul Shatter**
  (the same mention read by "they") and **Altar of the Brood**.
  **Consume's benched singular is unchanged**, and so is every other
  `Does` site: `agentIntro` reduces to `nomIntro` for a subject that is
  not a pass.
  Two earlier attempts are recorded as rejected: widening `countWord` to
  count `EachD ManyOf` (double-counts under `ForEachOf`, which introduces
  both the group and the member — breaks Tidal Flats and Case of the
  Gateway Express), and binding `Each` itself at `OneOf` (contradicts the
  landed `eachPlayerBindsAGroup` measurement).
- **The counted threshold over a possessor set** — re-measures at **6
  lines**, not 9 (the five Turbulent lands plus Lashwhip Predator),
  against 259 lines writing "your opponents control" at all. **No row was
  needed**: `CountOf` already takes a description and `ControlledBy
  (PlayerGroup YourOpponents)` already describes one, so the clause is
  independent and counted exactly as the acceptance asked. Benches
  **Turbulent Fen**.

### Ownership as a possessor axis, and the plural relational possessor

- **ONE `OwnedBy` predicate**, `Predicate bs Object`, serving the
  description and the possessor axis together. Grounds as the umbrella
  gave them: [CR#109.4] leaves an exiled card controlled by nobody and
  [CR#108.3] leaves the owner as the only possessor. It carries no
  `zoneAdmit` (ownership admits every zone where control admits two) and
  no `predRegime`. Benches **Umbris, Fear Manifest** (the 6-line exile
  family, re-measured at 6) and **Blight Herder**.
  **Deviation:** the name was already taken by the possessive-zone
  reader's `ZoneScope` constructor, and Idris 2 shadows rather than
  overloads. The zone-scope constructor renamed to `PossessedBy` (6
  sites); the READER is otherwise untouched, and the v1 `Semantics`
  module's own `OwnedBy` predicate is the precedent for the name.
- **The plural relational possessor** — `PossessorsOf ax grp`, the
  possessor derived MEMBER-WISE, gated plural so the singular spelling
  never reaches it. `badGroupOwner` keeps its refusal untouched, with the
  contrast now sharp: "their owner" asks for one referent two cards need
  not share [CR#108.3] and still has no row; "their owners" names the
  group's image under the relation and is as many players as the group has
  members. The axis is DATA rather than two constructors, which halves the
  Noun tables it pays.
  **Its seat is not where the umbrella expected.** Re-measured, 147
  occurrences write "their owners' [zone]" and 35 "their controllers'
  [zone]" — and almost all of them need no possessor at all: `DestOk`
  already rules that a moved card is routed to its owner's zone regardless
  of the sentence [CR#400.3], so "into their owners' graveyards" IS the
  bare zone (Blight Herder benches that way), and "don't untap during
  their controllers' untap steps" is `DoesntUntap`'s own period
  [CR#502.3], spelling and not a slot. The one open seat is the **entry
  control rider**, 22 supported lines: `CtrlSingular` renamed to
  `CtrlOverrideOk` and gained `PerMemberController`, so "return the exiled
  cards to the battlefield under their owners' control" writes while
  `badMoveRidersPluralController` keeps refusing a plural PLAYER WORD in
  the same slot [CR#110.2]. Benches **Open the Vaults**.
- **Freyalise's Winds and the missing `Owner` values**: recorded as a
  wrong premise. "During its controller's untap step" is the untap event's
  own period under [CR#502.3], on `CantMoreThan`'s already-landed ruling
  that the adverbial is spelling of the deed's recurrence — not a window
  possessor. No `Owner` value was added, and none is owed by these lines.

### The ownership predicate and the per-game history reads

- **The ownership condition** — re-measures at **9 lines**, not 5. Seven
  of them end in meld (the umbrella's five plus **Mishra, Claimed by Gix**
  and **Urza, Lord Protector**), and **the meld blocker stands: meld has
  no vocabulary and this round lands none of those seven.** The other two
  are not conditions at all but DESCRIPTIONS, and they are the non-melding
  carriers the pin asked for: **Obelisk of Undoing** ("Return target
  permanent you both own and control to your hand") is benched whole, and
  Remove Enchantments writes the same description under two further
  conjuncts. So the conjunction row reaches "both own and control" as two
  descriptions the moment ownership has one.
- **The Fallen** — benched, and the umbrella's "third reader" premise is
  corrected rather than built. [CR#120.1]'s two sides are two event names,
  and reading the clause at the VICTIM's voice (`DamageTaken`) puts the
  head in the subject seat and "it" in the complement — the shape
  `HappenedTo` has always had. `lookbackComplementOk`'s own table already
  anticipated the card, with the joined-kind row and a comment naming it.
  **No row was minted.**
- **The keyword-action event name** — answered for both cards at once, and
  the answer is that the name already exists. `VerbedAct` carries the
  label [CR#701.1] leaves open, so "as long as you've surveilled this
  turn" is an ordinary lookback; benches **Darkblade Agent**, and Eye of
  Duskmantle reads the same event. Yidaro is the same answer arriving
  negative: [CR#702.29c] defines "when you cycle this card" as "when you
  discard this card to pay an activation cost of a cycling ability", so
  cycling is **no keyword action** and a `VerbedAct "Cycle"` row would be a
  fiction. Yidaro counts a labelled COST PAYMENT and stays blocked in
  `PaysCost`'s neighbourhood.
- **Gollum** stays blocked on `EventSum`, unchanged.
- **The Frodos are no longer out of reach.** The trigger round's
  `verbFacts` row for the Ring's temptation makes "the Ring has tempted
  you N times this game" an ordinary `VerbedAct` count with the tempted
  player as its subject [CR#701.54d]; benched as
  `ringHasTemptedYouTwiceThisGame`. What the two cards still wait on is
  the Ring-BEARER possessive ("if Frodo is your Ring-bearer"): the
  designation is object-scoped and checked, so `HasDesignation RingBearer`
  says "is a Ring-bearer", and the possessive naming whose has no row.
- **The two per-game zeros survive**: "chosen this game" is 0 lines and
  "noted this game" is 0 lines (re-measured 2026-08-28), and every
  "first/second time" in the corpus remains turn-scoped.

### Routed items

- **Adventure faces re-measured.** 17 supported cards write "any other
  target" (not the umbrella's eleven, on this round's definition of the
  reading). Of those, the card SHAPE is no longer a blocker for three:
  Callous Sell-Sword // Burn Together (an Adventure, now `Adventurer`) and
  the two Sieges, Invasion of Tarkir and Invasion of Ulgrotha (now
  `Transforming`). What they wait on is each card's own remaining
  vocabulary, not the multiface shape.
- **The commander description** — built. `HasCardDesignation` reads a
  CARD-scoped designation, which `HasDesignation` cannot: [CR#903.3] makes
  the commander designation an attribute of the card rather than a
  characteristic of the object, and the scope gate is what parts the two
  rows. 37 supported lines head a phrase with the word. Benches **Bastion
  Protector** and the owned description **Master Chef** heads
  (`commanderCreaturesYouOwn`), which needed BOTH of this round's
  description rows.
- **`negatable (QualityNoun _)`** — scope fixed. [CR#105.1] empties the
  complement only for the DOMAINLESS quality noun; a domain-restricted one
  has every value the narrowing left out, so it negates.
- **The all-graveyards possessor** — **no row needed**: the
  possessive-zone reader already takes a plural player noun, so "all
  graveyards" is `PossessedBy (PlayerGroup AllPlayers)`. Benched inside
  **Open the Vaults**, alongside the bare-zone spelling the Lhurgoyf
  witness uses.
- **The attacked-by predicate** — built. `AttackedBy` describes the
  defender by the creature attacking it, kind-indexed on [CR#506.3]
  rather than split into two marked rows, and uniquifying on [CR#508.1b].
  Benches **Cavalcade of Calamity**; Raid Bombardment prints the same
  sentence. Tahngarth's player-relatum half stays routed elsewhere.
- **The bare CARD word and the permanent-card class** — both needed **no
  row**. [CR#109.2] names "card" among the four words that take a
  description out of the battlefield default, `headIsPlaceless` reads it,
  so "a creature card" is a conjunction (`creatureCardAnywhere`) and "a
  permanent card" is placeless (`permanentCardAnywhere`, measured by
  `permanentCardIsPlaceless`). Syr Konrad's arm 2, Disa, both Ultrons and
  the four permanent-card cards can spell the printed word now.
- **`castComplementOk (Just Battlefield)`** — **retired**, not
  re-grounded, because there is no rule to re-ground it on: [CR#601.2a]
  moves the card "from where it is" and excludes no zone and [CR#601.3]
  leaves the permission to whatever effect grants it, which is exactly the
  ground `playableFrom (Just Battlefield)` already stands on. The whole
  seven-row table went with it, and `playSourceOk`'s counterfactual arm
  now asks only about a written origin phrase.
  **Consequence, flagged:** `badFlashPermissionOnPermanent` (ProofsF) was
  that refusal's pin and is **retired with it**. Its stated ground
  ([CR#701.5b], "to cast a card is to cast it as a spell") does not say a
  battlefield-worded complement is unwritable; the refusal was a count.
- **`seedTy (HasSubtype s)`** — fixed: a subtype word is a head noun and
  [CR#205.3c] correlates it to one card type, so it projects that type.
  Rukarumel's `HostedRead` gate passes now. **Required a companion fix**:
  `negTypesOf` must not read a negated SUBTYPE as a negated TYPE, or
  "attacking creature that isn't a Demon" empties its own conjunction.
- **The characteristic LIST on `Compare`** — built. The slot is a
  `NonEmpty List Characteristic` read disjunctively, and `seedsUniform`'s
  refusal of three arms with different presuppositions stays correct
  because there are no longer three arms. `comparedTypes` empties the
  list's presupposition where one member presupposes nothing. Benches
  **Talion, the Kindly Lord** whole. 49 existing call sites moved to the
  one-element list.
- **The object partitive over a described group** — **decided and built**.
  The base of a partitive is not the base of "each of", so the two stopped
  sharing `groupMention`: "each of" fills a determiner position and its
  base must leave one open (so "each of all creatures" still refuses),
  while "from among" names the set a pick comes out of and admits the
  universal, whose members [CR#608.2d] fixes as the effect is applied.
  **86 supported lines** write "from among [a description]" (a bigger
  family than the 23 the routing recorded). All three standing partitive
  pins are untouched, `groupMention (PlayerGroup _)` is still False, and
  the partitive widening the "one of your opponents" section refused is
  not reintroduced — this is a different gate at a different reader.
  Benches `creatureSpellFromAmongExiled`.

## Remainders

- **"All X from among them"** — re-measures at **10 lines**, not 8
  (Animist's Awakening, Muxus, Tezzeret Master of the Bridge and seven
  more). Not built. "All" is not a `Quantity` and should not become one:
  a quantity value for it would make "all target creatures" writable and
  duplicate `AllOf`. The shape it wants is a partitive whose count
  position is the universal — one row beside `SomeOf`, not a widening of
  `Quantity`. Owed.
- **Yidaro** — blocked on counting a labelled cost payment, not on an
  event name. Owed to whichever round opens `PaysCost`.
- **Gollum** — blocked on `EventSum`, unchanged.
- **The Frodos** — blocked on the Ring-BEARER possessive alone.
- **The meld seven** — Gisela, Graf Rats, Hanweir Battlements, Titania,
  Vanille, Mishra and Urza all end in meld and land no card.
- **Rukarumel** — its `HostedRead` gate is fixed; the card is not benched,
  and what remains of it was not measured here.
- **Remove Enchantments** — writes the ownership description under two
  further conjuncts (Aura attachment) and is not benched.
- **Sub-round 2's** — "one of your opponents", the player-headed
  comparison, the negated existential over players, the snapshot rider,
  the life exchange, `Predicate.Opponent`'s team form and its of-`You`
  restriction (Shared Fate), and the distributive possessive ("each player
  … their own") are untouched here by design.
