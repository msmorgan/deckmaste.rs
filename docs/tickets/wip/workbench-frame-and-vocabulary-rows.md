# Reach the card frames' edges and buy the cheap surfaces and vocabulary rows

The printed card frames — loyalty and Saga — have measured residues at their
edges, and beside them sit two piles of cheap work: surfaces whose semantics
already exist, and vocabulary rows that are rows rather than constructions. They
are one claimable unit because none of them is a design question: each is a row,
a spelling, or a recorded refusal.

## The loyalty frame's four unwritten positions

The loyalty frame writes the ability's cost and nothing around it. Four measured
entries sit outside it: the activation as a watchable event, the two printed
boxes (loyalty and defense) with the one place a box becomes language, and the
two positions where the loyalty symbol is written outside a cost. They are one
sub-area because they are one frame's edges.

### The loyalty activation as an event — 6 supported cards

"Whenever you activate a loyalty ability of enchanted planeswalker", and the
Talent cycle's remaining blocker. Six cards, not the two an earlier brief
carried: Chandra's Regulator, Keral Keep Disciples, Elspeth's Talent, Rowan's
Talent, Repeated Reverberation, and The Chain Veil's negative form ("if you
didn't activate a loyalty ability of a planeswalker this turn"). That is over the
minting bar.

No `GameEvent` row watches an **activation** at all — the event vocabulary
watches things happening to objects and to players, and this watches a player
*using an ability* — so it is a **row and not a cell**. Elspeth's Talent and
Rowan's Talent are its whole-card witnesses and both are one gap from landing.

### The two printed boxes, and the one line that makes one of them language

The **starting-loyalty** box and the **defense** box are printed card parts the
container carries no field for; both were deliberate non-purchases and neither is
blocked by anything else. What makes the loyalty box an entry now is the two
supported lines that **set** it on a token copy — "except it's not legendary and
its starting loyalty is 1" (Jace, Mirror Mage) and "The copy isn't legendary and
has starting loyalty X" (Ob Nixilis, the Adversary). Both ride a copy clause,
both confirmed at one line apiece, and `CopyExcept` carries the legendary refusal
and **not** the loyalty one — so the pair belongs with the copy exception kinds.

The defense box has no such line. The battle row landed and Invasion of Dominaria
pays for it, but only its **front face** and only because the Siege reminder is
not a line; all **36** supported battle cards are transforming Sieges, so the
defense box and the transform back face arrive together whenever either is
scheduled. The defense number is the same shape as loyalty, which is the same
shape as the planeswalker's declined box.

### The loyalty symbol outside a cost — 2 cards, both under the bar alone

- Carth the Lion writes it as an **additional cost a static confers**:
  "Planeswalkers' loyalty abilities you activate cost an additional [+1] to
  activate" — [CR#606.5]'s own example card, and a cost *modification* rather
  than a cost, which is the total-cost pipeline [CR#601.2f] this vocabulary
  ledgers whole.
- Comet, Stellar Pup writes it as an **effect**, four times, inside die-roll
  outcome clauses ("1 or 2 — [+2], then create two 1/1 green Squirrel creature
  tokens").

The two shapes want different things — a cost-shift vocabulary and a counter
clause — and neither has a second card. Recorded at their counts; this pair waits
for company rather than for a design, and a round that opens the frame should not
mint rows for them on this evidence alone.

## The Saga frame's unbuilt neighbours: read ahead, the named chapter, the transform chapter

The Saga frame itself landed (`GameEvent.ChapterMark` under the ordinary
`Triggered`, `ChapterDefaults`, `CardChapters`, `Subtype.Saga`, `CounterKind.Lore`;
History of Benalia, Vault 75: Middle School, Founding of Omashu, Keldon Warcaller
whole). What is left is a counted list of neighbours and no design question about
the frame.

Authority: [The kind index joins; union marking is spelling](../../decisions/kind-index-joins-union-marking-is-spelling.md)
— two of read ahead's carriers are blocked on coordinated recipients/subjects;
those are noun-coordination questions, not kind-unification ones.

### READ AHEAD — 10 supported cards, [CR#702.155a,714.3b]

One `Keyword` catalog row and its usual six cells. The catalog's discipline mints
it on the round a witness prints it, and NO WITNESS IS CHEAP — each of the ten
needs machinery from an unbuilt family for at least one chapter:

The Elder Dragon War (a cross-kind coordinated damage recipient AND a "that many"
readback over a discard), Founding the Third Path (cast-without-paying, a
copy-a-card verb), The Weatherseed Treaty (domain), Urza Assembles the Titans
(the loyalty-activation permission), Braids's Frightful Return and The Cruelty of
Gix (reveal-and-choose), The Phasing of Zhalfir (phasing), The World Spell
(look-at-N-and-take), Yotia Declares War (the reflexive "When you do"), Love Song
of Night and Day (a coordinated you-and-opponent subject). ALSO WANTED BY Barbara
Wright, whose "Sagas you control have read ahead" is a grant of the same keyword.

### The NAMED CHAPTER — 38 supported lines over 24 cards

The Final Fantasy Sagas write a name inside the striation after the dash — "I,
II, III — Pain — You draw a card and you lose 1 life" (Summon: Anima) and 36 more
across the Summon cycle and the Dominant double-faced cards. Two other
placements exist unsupported: Five Stages of Grief writes its names BEFORE the
dash ("I Denial —") and Summon: Magus Sisters writes them inside modal BULLETS
("• Defense! — Put a shield counter on target creature").

The 38th line is the giveaway: The Weatherseed Treaty's "III — Domain — Target
creature you control gets +X/+X", where the name is a real ABILITY WORD. So this
is the ability-word idiom ("Boast — ", "History Teacher — ") at a new carrier, and
measuring the two together is the better-shaped round — this grammar spells no
ability word anywhere yet.

### The transform chapter — 29 supported lines, one sentence

"Exile this Saga, then return it to the battlefield transformed under your
control" — the double-faced Sagas' whole mechanism (43 supported transform Saga
faces, 29 fronts and 14 backs). An ordinary chapter EFFECT needing nothing of the
frame; what it wants is the transform verb and the
exile-and-return-under-your-control compound. The largest single unwritten
chapter sentence there is.

### The Saga CREATURE's second text box — [CR#714.1a], 18 bare keyword lines

Over the Summon cycle: Flying 6, Menace 2, "Flying, lifelink" 2, "Ward {2}",
"Reach, trample", "Trample, haste", "Flying, haste", First strike, Indestructible,
Haste, Vigilance. The frame gate already admits them (probed green), so this costs
only whatever `Keyword` rows the chosen witness prints — but the cycle's chapter
effects are mana abilities and modal lists, so no Summon card is close. The other
6 non-chapter lines on supported Sagas are modal bullets under a chapter, not
second-box abilities.

### The MODAL chapter — 2 cards

Life of Toshiro Umezawa's "I, II — Choose one —" and Summon: Magus Sisters' "I,
II, III — Choose one at random —". The mode list is `chooseOne`'s and needs
nothing new; both cards are blocked on their other lines.

### The Saga's own tally as a READ — writable today, unclaimed

`CountersOn Lore (AsType Enchantment This {sub = Just Saga})` probed green, so
"the number of lore counters on this Saga" costs nothing. The lines that write it
(The Triumph of Anax, Genesis of the Daleks, Summon: Esper Valigarmanda, Mind
Unbound, Scroll of the Masters, Tom Bombadil, Chong and Lily) each want their own
consuming effect, which is where their blockers sit.

### Do not build

- Two card-level laws, measured and deliberately NOT gated. Chapter COMPLETENESS
  (a Saga's numerals cover 1..N exactly once) holds on all 223 supported cards
  and is still declined, because [CR#714.2d] and [CR#714.4] both provide for the
  Saga it would forbid. Printed ORDER is refused by an attested card: BLINK
  prints "I, III —" above "II, IV —".
- The final chapter ability as an EVENT, 2 supported lines and [CR#714.2e]'s own
  term — "Whenever the final chapter ability of a Saga you control triggers"
  (Historian's Boon) and "… resolves" (Narci, Fable Singer). This is the
  ability-class subject gap at a third site (`Kind` has no ability sort) and
  belongs to that round, which must also tell the two verbs apart. Recorded here
  only so the cross-link is visible from the Saga side.

## The four surfaces whose semantics already exist

Four measured residues where the semantics are built and only a SURFACE is
missing. They are one sub-area because each is a spelling job against an existing
term, not a new construction.

- **Clause ellipsis**, including Galvanic Blast's replacement clause writing no
  recipient.
- **Effect disjunction.**
- **The bare generic plural** (finding 223).
- **The definite determiner over a DESCRIPTION** — what "the card exiled with
  this artifact" needs, and what makes finding 206's second surface unwritable.

A new semantics row in this sub-area is a sign the entry was misdiagnosed.

## The cheap vocabulary rows left standing

Five measured vocabulary gaps, all cheap, all rows rather than constructions.
Two of them unblock benched cards on their own; one is deliberately NOT to be
bought and is recorded here so it is not re-derived as an oversight.

### The nominal possessor tier

What the possessor round left: "enchanted player's upkeep", "its controller's
end step", and the Curse and Aura mass — a possessive over a NOUN, where that
round minted quantifier WORDS (`Owner`). It wants an anaphoric-noun possessor,
which is also what would let a quantifier possessor be an ANTECEDENT — the gap
that costs `EachOpponents` its bench positive despite 33 corpus lines (finding
330).

### The activation window's boundary-relative forms

"During your turn, before attackers are declared" (19 lines) and the bare
"before attackers are declared" (24 lines). `DuringPart` names a part to be
INSIDE; these name a point to be BEFORE, which is a different slot.

## Consumption boundary

`idris/src/Experimental.idr` (the loyalty frame, `CopyExcept`, the copy
exception kinds, the container's card parts, `GameEvent.ChapterMark`,
`ChapterDefaults`, `CardChapters`, `Keyword` rows, the transform verb and move
compounds, `DuringPart`, the activation window, the creation check and the
linkage, and only as much else as a surface needs — a new semantics row for one
of the four surfaces is a sign the entry was misdiagnosed),
`idris/src/Experimental/Events.idr` (`GameEvent` and its readers) for the
activation row, `idris/src/Experimental/Words.idr` (keyword and chapter-name
spelling, `Owner`, `TurnPart`, the possessor and participle words, the determiner
words), `idris/src/Experimental/Macros.idr` (the spellings), the pin modules
`idris/src/Experimental/Proofs*.idr`, evidence bench
`idris/src/Experimental/Cards.idr`. No Rust crate. The vocabulary rows are also
candidates for the Rust side once the grammar shape is settled; nothing here
requires a crate change first.

## Acceptance

- The activation lands as its own event row, not as a cell on an existing one,
  and Elspeth's Talent and Rowan's Talent are re-checked against their remaining
  gap.
- If a printed box is bought, both boxes are decided together and the battle's
  back face is answered with the defense number.
- The two loyalty-symbol positions stay recorded at their counts unless a second
  card exists for the shape being minted.
- Whatever Saga work is taken benches a real card; the read-ahead row lands only
  with a witness that prints it.
- The named chapter is measured together with the ability-word idiom before a
  shape is chosen; the three placements are all accounted for.
- Neither Saga card-level law is gated.
- Each of the four surfaces writes with the existing semantics term; nothing new
  is added to the core to spell them.
- Galvanic Blast's recipient-less replacement clause writes.
- Finding 206's second surface writes, or the round records why the definite
  determiner still refuses it.
- The nominal possessor admits an anaphoric noun, and `EachOpponents` gets its
  bench positive on the back of it.
- The boundary-relative window is its own slot, not folded into `DuringPart`.
- The counter NAME tails stay unbought and finding 229's bar stands.
- `idris/scripts/build` PASS, no witness lost, no pin silently passing.

Standard constraints apply.
