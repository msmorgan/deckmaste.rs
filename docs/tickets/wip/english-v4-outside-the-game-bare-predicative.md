# english-v4: *outside the game* as a bare predicative

The noun *outside the game* is `[Count, Mass]` (`nouns.ron`), so that it
stands bare after *from* (*a card you own from outside the game*). That
mass reading also admits it as a bare predicative: *If it's outside the
game, ...* reads twice, as the Preposition Phrase and as the noun. The
owner ruled that the noun appears only after *from* and in zone-noun
coordinations, with the preposition elsewhere. A bare zone noun is no
predicative (*it's graveyard*).

## Change

Restrict the bare noun to the Complement of *from*. CGEL treats this as a
fixed frame whose nouns "are severely restricted" (Ch. 5 §8.5(b),
pp. 409–410). Model that restriction lexically: a noun admitted bare in a
preposition's fixed frame. Do not use an open frame or a ban aimed at this
one noun.

Rejected (`english-v4-rescan-s7-hygiene`): the noun made count-only, with
an open `Nominal` frame on *from*. That doubled 26 units (*from combat*,
*from Chandra*) and lost Legion Angel, Living Wish and Granted.

## Done when

- A judgment pins *it's outside the game* to the Preposition Phrase alone
  (`readings 1`).
- Legion Angel, Invasion of Arcavios and Turtles Forever keep their
  judgments, and no face is lost.

Standard constraints apply.
