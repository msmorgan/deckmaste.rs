## Findings and dispositions

| Finding from the initial review | Resolution and checked evidence |
|---|---|
| Document categories coordinated as ordinary phrases; binary editorial joins duplicated association and singleton shapes | Explicit coordination distribution; flat typed collections. `DocumentCollections` checks three-element groups, category exclusions, empty sections and canonical singleton arity. |
| Trigger-shaped text could not share a paragraph with later sentences or modes | Initial adverbials are clause grammar. Shared ordinary/activated bodies admit multi-sentence and modal combinations in `DocumentCollections`. |
| Nested quotation added a second terminal period | `Surface.finishSentence` traverses closing quotes. `Interactions.Quotation.deep_quote_written` checks the exact nested string; preceding quotation assertions remain. |
| Nested mobiles had only an abstract example | `Interactions.NestedScope` derives four pairwise-distinct readings and their quotation embedding. `ScopeInteractions` supplies actual scope equivalence and preservation witnesses. |
| Arbitrary claims and keys could choose homographs or merge unrelated readings | `RolePreference` derives local role competition from declarations. `Analysis` adds evidenced identity preference and admits only feature/dependency-checked candidates. Its class is generated by grammatical moves, not supplied arbitrary keys. `AnalysisInteractions` checks actual selection and packing. |
| Partial frames lacked real syntax and alternate anchorings | `JudgeFrameIn.coordinate` repeats a nonempty frame portion. `FrameInteractions` checks paired arguments and shared Objects. `FrameScope.Witnesses` derives both alternate boundary readings, proves identical surfaces and no boundary preference; `AnalysisInteractions.Frames.complete_packing` retains both through the final candidate contract. |
| Abstract right-periphery/role boundaries had no syntax projection | `FrameScope.projectHost` and `frameHost` read actual syntax plus declared frame items. Nonfinal branches are excluded; marked roles are opaque and fixed markers transparent. First-eligible uniqueness is inherited over this projection. |
| Mixed person/number agreement was absent | `AgreementInteractions` checks mixed additive agreement, alternative proximity and wrong-agreement exclusions with positive lexical licenses. Inverted clause syntax is not claimed. |
| Countability, modifier ordering and temporal NPs lacked carriers | `Features` refines the existing syntax. `FeatureInteractions` checks mass quantities, numeral rejection, Targeting Marker order and passive temporal adjuncts without adding Objects to the passive frame. |
| Relative forms and extraction boundaries were unspecified | `Dependencies` tracks category/relation at exposed positions and constrains boundaries. `DependencyInteractions` checks zero-object versus zero-subject relatives, which, possessive fronting with independent agreement, pied-piping, and adjunct-gap rejection. |
| Ellipsis recursively embedded arbitrary antecedent trees | Ellipsis is a form/voice omission checked against paragraph context. `EllipsisInteractions` checks accessibility, future/form exclusions, quotation isolation, and a grammatical inline reminder. |
| Shared heads had no explicit discharge mechanism | `rightNodeRaising` fills shared gaps with following material. `SharingInteractions` checks inhabited shared-head and determiner alternatives, including feature/dependency checks for the head case. |
| Semicolon keyword lines and sentence-final supplementary punctuation were absent | `BoundaryInteractions` checks an exact semicolon line, rejects a second singleton separator shape, and proves a complete sentence ending in a supplementary relative without a dangling comma. |
| Source normalization, notation and layout validation were conflated | The lexical/source contract is explicit. Word-payload examples distinguish lexical apostrophes from whitespace and quote boundaries. Notation may be declared data; game/layout validity and physical typography are outside English. No whole-Lexicon source validator is claimed. |

## Source checks

The style guide remains independent structural evidence; it was not edited to
fit the implementation. Countability/determiner and relative-form decisions
were checked against Cambridge's [determiners and noun types](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/uk/grammar/british-grammar/determiners-and-types-of-noun)
and [relative pronouns](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/relative-pronouns/).
Agreement uses [Purdue's subject–verb agreement](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/grammar/subject_verb_agreement.html)
and the Canadian government's [compound subjects with “or”](https://our-languages.canada.ca/en/writing-tips-plus/verb-agreement-compound-subjects-with-or),
including inverted order. The latter motivates a tested projection, not an
unimplemented inverted-clause claim.

Supported corpus inspection and exact card lookups supplied conformance
checks, not grammar guards. Browbeat illustrates an overt auxiliary with
omitted VP material. Renegade Doppelganger disproved the draft assumption
that reminder context must be isolated: its reminder refers back to preceding
text. The draft was corrected before commitment; no committed assertion was
removed. The inherited Arwen frame-pair ruling requires both bracketings to
pack; the tracked witnesses use synthetic vocabulary to test that structure.
No named-card census or coverage gain is claimed.

## Exact proof boundaries

`Analysis.Reading` is the final candidate interface. Base `Admissible` alone
checks composition and realization; it does not certify feature distribution
or extraction constraints. Selection and packing over the final interface
cannot bypass those checks. Every intermediate in its scope chain is another
checked candidate. Finite survivor existence and enumeration independence do
not imply one survivor, a complete parse forest, or global freedom from ties.

The original attachment-only host invariant remains true in its original
module. Reanchoring and shared-head/determiner moves extend the relation in
`FrameScope`; lexical identity and anchor count are proved for that extension.
Those invariants are not sufficient conditions for equivalence. Only an
explicit grammatical move chain licenses a common class. The abstract
flat/nested fence and earlier inhabited association exclusions remain intact.

Preference is currently evidenced for one Object plus a marked role, and for
an atomic supplied identity versus a lexical word over the same surface,
with the implemented surrounding contexts. The counts used in termination
proofs do not authorize new preferences. General role sequences, more scope
moves and more grammatical families must follow the elaboration obligations
in the design before their production migrations are considered ready.

The context model establishes a bounded grammatical recoverability fragment;
it does not resolve game references or prove discourse adequacy. Extraction
checks are a constrained prototype, not a complete English island theory.
Lexical allomorphs, source normalization and full notation/template populations
remain explicit production correspondence obligations. None are silently
reclassified as the post-migration card tail.

## Validation

All 117 pre-review theorem declarations remain by name, with their intended
outcomes retained. The final source contains 297 named theorem declarations:
180 added, none removed or ignored. Re-spelled baseline statements are the two
ellipsis assertions (`elliptic_antecedent`, `elliptic_surface`) `nested_reminder_rejected`, and `qualification_sites_excluded`; wrapper
proofs and generic derivations were adapted without changing their asserted
outcomes. No assertion was restored from deletion or given a blocker/ignore.
The project split moved the original modules; Semantics source was not changed.

The complete English warning-as-error gate passes 29 jobs. LSP diagnostics and sequential selected axiom audits are clean. The source
scan flags only the inspected local `Candidate` notation in `Analysis`; it
abbreviates the complete checked reading type and introduces no axiom. A separate import-based
audit scans every compiled English theorem, including generated/private
ones, for unexpected axiom dependencies. Concurrent same-file LSP verification
initially returned inconsistent `sorryAx` reports; sequential checks and the
compiled audit show only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
That concurrent output is not used as proof evidence.

Both independent Lake projects are checked at landing, with cross-project
imports required to fail. Production Rust and corpus coverage are unchanged;
this ticket follows the workbench validation exception in the accepted
decision. The remaining migration obligations are explicit in the design and
wayfinder. There is no residual user decision requiring a grilling round.
